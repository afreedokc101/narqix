## The shift in one view

1. **Prepare** (before your scheduled shift starts)
2. **Check in** with your campaign's check-in method
3. **Huddle** and plan
4. **Work the blocks** in priority order, following US time zones from east to west
5. **Close out** every record you touched
6. **Report and check out**

## Bangladesh time windows

Dhaka is 6 hours ahead of UTC all year and does not change its clocks. The US does. That means your shift moves by one hour twice a year.

- **US daylight time (summer):** from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November. In 2026 it ends on Sunday, November 1. In 2027 it starts on Sunday, March 14.
- **US standard time (winter):** the rest of the year. Most US zones are one hour further behind Dhaka, so everything happens one hour later for you.
- **Arizona** (most of the state) and **Hawaii** do not change their clocks.

### US business hours (9 AM to 6 PM local) in Dhaka time

| US time zone | Summer (US daylight time) | Winter (US standard time) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern | 7 PM to 4 AM | 8 PM to 5 AM |
| Central | 8 PM to 5 AM | 9 PM to 6 AM |
| Mountain | 9 PM to 6 AM | 10 PM to 7 AM |
| Arizona | 10 PM to 7 AM | 10 PM to 7 AM |
| Pacific | 10 PM to 7 AM | 11 PM to 8 AM |
| Alaska | 11 PM to 8 AM | 12 AM (midnight) to 9 AM |
| Hawaii | 1 AM to 10 AM | 1 AM to 10 AM |

### Federal calling window (8 AM to 9 PM local) in Dhaka time

| US time zone | Summer: first call / last call | Winter: first call / last call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern | 6 PM / 7 AM | 7 PM / 8 AM |
| Central | 7 PM / 8 AM | 8 PM / 9 AM |
| Mountain | 8 PM / 9 AM | 9 PM / 10 AM |
| Arizona | 9 PM / 10 AM | 9 PM / 10 AM |
| Pacific | 9 PM / 10 AM | 10 PM / 11 AM |

Some states set shorter windows or limit call attempts per day. The dialer enforces your campaign's exact rules. These tables help you plan; they never give you permission to override the dialer.

Your campaign sets your actual scheduled shift. Common patterns:

| Pattern | Summer (Dhaka) | Winter (Dhaka) | Covers |

## Before your shift

- [ ]  Power backup charged (UPS or inverter) and mobile data backup ready in case of load-shedding or a line outage
- [ ]  Approved device updated, headset tested, quiet private workspace
- [ ]  Nothing on your desk or screen that does not belong to work
- [ ]  Logged in to CRM, dialer, texting platform (if your role uses it), email, team channel and script library
- [ ]  Check in with your campaign's check-in method on time

## Huddle (first 10 to 15 minutes)

- Read overnight notes in the team channel: inbound leads, merchant replies, funder updates, stips
- Review your queue: callbacks due, docs pending, stips pending, renewals due
- Your lead shares the focus for the shift and any rule changes (for example, a new state cleared or paused)
- Write your top three goals for the shift

## Work blocks by role

| Role | Priority order every block |
| --- | --- |
| Lead Qualifier | New inbound leads (under 5 minutes), then scheduled callbacks, then application follow-ups, then cleared outbound lists by time zone |
| Application Assistant and Document Chaser | Reminders that must reach merchants early, then new handoffs, then checking new uploads, then chasing gaps |
| Funding Processor and File Analyst | Stips pending, then scrubbing complete files, then packages, then submissions before US end of day |
| Renewal and Follow-Up Rep | Docs pending, then funded check-ins, then renewal watch, then declined re-checks, then nurture |

Every hour, every role: check for new inbound messages and opt-outs.

## Breaks

Follow your campaign's break schedule. Before a break, set your dialer status correctly and make sure no merchant is waiting on a reply you promised within the next few minutes.

## If power or internet fails

1. Switch to backup power or mobile data.
2. Post in the team channel within 5 minutes: what failed, when, and your expected return.
3. If you were on a call that dropped, call back as soon as you are reconnected (if still inside the merchant's calling window) and apologise briefly.
4. If you cannot reconnect within 15 minutes, tell your lead so your queue can be covered.
5. Log the downtime in your end-of-shift report.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never switch to a personal phone, personal email or a messaging app to keep working during an outage. Never take merchant documents offline to "work on later". Wait for approved systems.

</div>

## Close out

Before you report:

- [ ]  Every lead or file you touched has a status, a disposition and a next task with a due time
- [ ]  Every opt-out you received is marked in the CRM, dialer and texting platform
- [ ]  Every promise you made ("I'll email you in 10 minutes") is done
- [ ]  No merchant document is anywhere except the secure vault

## End-of-shift report

Post in the team channel in this format:

> **End of shift · (YOUR NAME) · (ROLE) · (DATE)**
Worked: (NUMBER) leads or files
Results: qualified (N), apps signed (N), files completed (N), submitted (N), stips cleared (N) (use the lines for your role)
Opt-outs processed: (N)
Escalations: (SHORT LIST OR NONE)
Blockers and downtime: (SHORT LIST OR NONE)
First priority for next shift: (ONE LINE)
> 

Then check out with your campaign's check-in method.

## Weekly rhythm

- Call QA review with your lead (recorded calls scored against the quality checklist)
- Calibration session: the team scores the same call or scrub sheet and compares
- Review of rule changes: states cleared or paused, script updates, funder matrix changes
- Personal KPI review against campaign targets
