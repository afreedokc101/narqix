## Rules for every document

1. **Secure channel only.** Documents come through the secure upload link or the CRM portal. Never personal email or messaging apps.
2. **Bank-original.** Statements are PDFs downloaded from the bank's website or app. No photos, screenshots, scans of printouts or editable files.
3. **Complete.** Every page, including blank pages and the last page. Check "page X of Y".
4. **Current.** The most recent full months, plus month-to-date where required. IDs not expired.
5. **Matching.** Names, addresses and account numbers match across documents. If not, ask and note.
6. **Named.** Legal name, document type, period. Example: (LEGAL NAME) Statement (YEAR-MONTH).pdf
7. **Masked in notes.** In the CRM, write only the last 4 digits of Social Security and account numbers.

## The core business package

Most business products start with this package. Product toggles below say what to add or change.

- [ ]  Signed application: legal name, DBA, EIN, business address, start date, industry, every owner of 20% or more with ownership percentage, Social Security number, date of birth, home address, signature and authorization to share with funders
- [ ]  Business bank statements: last 4 full months plus month-to-date, every page
- [ ]  Driver's license or state ID, front and back, for each signer
- [ ]  Voided business check or a bank letter showing account and routing numbers
- [ ]  Card processing statements if most sales are card sales
- [ ]  For larger requests: most recent business tax return or year-to-date profit and loss statement

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: "month-to-date" is the item merchants forget most, and it is the item funders care about most, because it shows what happened since the last full statement. Ask for it by name and explain why: "It shows the funder your most recent activity, which usually helps you."

</div>

## Product checklists

<details class="toggle"><summary>Merchant Cash Advance</summary>

- [ ]  Core business package
- [ ]  Card processing statements (last 3 to 4 months) if card-heavy
- [ ]  Merchant's own list of any current advances (funder, payment, frequency)

**Quality checks:** statements consecutive with no gap month; month-to-date dated within the last few days; processing deposits on statements roughly match processing statements.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>MCA Bailout</summary>

- [ ]  Core business package
- [ ]  Every current advance contract
- [ ]  Payoff letter or current balance statement for each advance
- [ ]  Position list: funder, payment amount, frequency, start date, estimated balance
- [ ]  Total weekly advance payments, in the merchant's words

**Quality checks:** number of contracts matches number of funder debits on statements; payoff letters are recent; any default notice is flagged to the desk lead immediately.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Working Capital Loan</summary>

- [ ]  Core business package
- [ ]  Business debt schedule if existing debt is significant
- [ ]  Year-to-date profit and loss statement for larger requests

**Quality checks:** debts on the schedule appear as debits on statements, and debits on statements appear on the schedule.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Revenue Based Financing</summary>

- [ ]  Signed application
- [ ]  6 months of business bank statements plus month-to-date
- [ ]  Driver's license, front and back
- [ ]  Voided business check
- [ ]  Sales platform or processor reports for online sellers

**Quality checks:** six consecutive months; seasonal swings explained in the notes.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Term Loans</summary>

- [ ]  Core business package (6 months of statements)
- [ ]  2 years of business tax returns, all schedules
- [ ]  2 years of personal tax returns for each owner of 20% or more
- [ ]  Year-to-date profit and loss statement and balance sheet
- [ ]  Business debt schedule

**Quality checks:** returns are signed or show e-file confirmation; the business name and EIN on returns match the application.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Lines of Credit</summary>

- [ ]  Core business package
- [ ]  Business license or EIN letter
- [ ]  List of existing credit lines and balances (if any)

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Equipment Lending</summary>

- [ ]  Signed application
- [ ]  Equipment quote or invoice: seller, make, model, year, price, and serial number if used
- [ ]  Equipment specs or brochure
- [ ]  3 months of business bank statements
- [ ]  1 to 2 years of business tax returns for larger amounts
- [ ]  Driver's license and voided business check

**Quality checks:** the seller is a real, separate business; used equipment has a clear title or proof of ownership from the seller.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Invoice Factoring</summary>

- [ ]  Signed application
- [ ]  Accounts receivable aging report (who owes what, and how long)
- [ ]  Customer list with contact details and balances
- [ ]  Sample invoices
- [ ]  Proof of delivery or completed work for sample invoices
- [ ]  Payment terms with main customers
- [ ]  3 months of business bank statements
- [ ]  Entity documents and EIN letter
- [ ]  Driver's license
- [ ]  Disclosure of any existing lender with a lien on receivables

**Quality checks:** invoices are to businesses or government, not consumers; aging report totals match invoice samples; customer payments appear on statements.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Purchase Order Factoring</summary>

- [ ]  Signed application
- [ ]  Copy of the purchase order from the buyer
- [ ]  Supplier quote or invoice and supplier payment terms
- [ ]  Buyer contact information
- [ ]  Margin breakdown (sale price minus supplier cost and shipping)
- [ ]  3 months of business bank statements
- [ ]  Accounts receivable aging report
- [ ]  Articles of incorporation or EIN letter
- [ ]  Driver's license

**Quality checks:** the PO is signed and dated by the buyer; delivery date is realistic; margin is 15% or more.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>0% Business Credit Cards</summary>

- [ ]  Signed application
- [ ]  Driver's license, front and back
- [ ]  Last 3 months of personal bank statements
- [ ]  Proof of business (EIN letter or articles)
- [ ]  Signed personal credit report authorization (soft pull first, per program)
- [ ]  Proof of income
- [ ]  Signed engagement terms showing any fee, provided by the company
- [ ]  Signed acknowledgment that card applications may involve hard inquiries, that cards usually require a personal guarantee, and that the promotional rate ends

**Quality checks:** the acknowledgment is signed before any application is submitted. No acknowledgment, no submission.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>SBA 7(a) Loan</summary>

- [ ]  SBA borrower information form for the business and each owner (recent, as required)
- [ ]  Personal financial statement for each owner of 20% or more and each guarantor
- [ ]  3 years of business tax returns or year-end financial statements
- [ ]  3 years of personal tax returns for each owner
- [ ]  Interim profit and loss statement and balance sheet (recent, as required)
- [ ]  Business debt schedule
- [ ]  3 months of business bank statements
- [ ]  Business plan or loan purpose narrative; projections for newer businesses
- [ ]  ID and resume for each owner
- [ ]  Purchase agreement if buying a business or property
- [ ]  Affiliate business financials if owners have other businesses
- [ ]  Any bankruptcy, litigation or judgment documents

**Quality checks:** dates on interim financials and forms are within the program's freshness window (the processing team confirms current rules).

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Credit Repair</summary>

- [ ]  Credit reports from all 3 bureaus
- [ ]  Government-issued ID
- [ ]  Proof of address
- [ ]  Proof of Social Security number
- [ ]  Signed written contract and disclosures, provided by the company

**Quality checks:** no work begins and nothing is promised before the company's contract is signed and any cancellation period has passed.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Mergers &amp; Acquisitions</summary>

- [ ]  Signed letter of intent
- [ ]  Target business: 3 years of tax returns and profit and loss statements, current balance sheet
- [ ]  Buyer: personal financial statement, 2 years of tax returns, 3 months of bank statements (proof of equity)
- [ ]  Purchase agreement (when drafted)
- [ ]  Buyer resume or industry experience summary

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Fix and Flip</summary>

- [ ]  Purchase agreement
- [ ]  Line-item scope of work and rehab budget, with timeline
- [ ]  Property photos
- [ ]  Contractor bids and contractor information
- [ ]  Comparable sales supporting the after-repair value
- [ ]  3 months of bank statements and proof of funds
- [ ]  Real estate experience summary (properties bought and sold)
- [ ]  Entity documents and EIN if buying in an LLC
- [ ]  Driver's license
- [ ]  Exit plan (sell or refinance)

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>DSCR Loans</summary>

- [ ]  Driver's license and credit authorization
- [ ]  Signed leases or rent roll
- [ ]  12 months of rent history (refinances)
- [ ]  Market rent analysis if vacant; short-term rental income reports if applicable
- [ ]  Property insurance declaration or quote
- [ ]  Purchase agreement (purchases) or current mortgage statement (refinances)
- [ ]  2 months of bank statements showing reserves
- [ ]  Entity documents and good standing if an LLC

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Residential Mortgages</summary>

Handled by the licensed team only. Your part ends at capturing interest and contact details.

For reference, the licensed team typically requests: government-issued ID, 2 years of W-2s or 1099s, 2 years of tax returns, 30 days of pay stubs, 3 months of bank statements, and a purchase contract or current mortgage statement.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Commercial Real Estate</summary>

- [ ]  Rent roll
- [ ]  3 years of property operating statements and the trailing 12 months
- [ ]  Personal financial statement for each sponsor
- [ ]  3 years of personal tax returns
- [ ]  Schedule of real estate owned
- [ ]  Purchase agreement (purchases) or current mortgage statement (refinances)
- [ ]  Entity documents
- [ ]  Sponsor resume or experience summary

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Ground Up Construction</summary>

- [ ]  Architectural plans and site layout
- [ ]  Itemized construction budget with contractor quotes and a contingency line
- [ ]  Draw schedule and phase timeline
- [ ]  General contractor agreement and license
- [ ]  Proof of land ownership or purchase contract
- [ ]  Building permits or permit status, and zoning confirmation
- [ ]  Builder's risk insurance quote
- [ ]  Personal financial statement and schedule of real estate owned
- [ ]  Bank statements showing liquidity
- [ ]  Entity documents
- [ ]  Completed projects list and exit plan

</details>

## Common document problems and how to fix them

| Problem | What to say |
| --- | --- |
| Photo of a printed statement | "Thank you. The funder needs the PDF straight from your bank's website or app. It usually takes two minutes: log in, go to statements, download." |
| Missing pages | "Your statement says 6 pages and we have 4. Could you download the full file again?" |
| Missing month-to-date | "Could you also download your activity from the 1st of this month to today? Most banks call it a transaction history or activity export." |
| Expired ID | "Your license on file has expired. Do you have the renewed one?" |
| Name mismatch | "The statement shows a different business name. Is that your DBA, or a different account?" |
| Personal account used for business | "Do you have a separate business account? If not, I'll note it, and the team will tell you how it affects the file." |

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never accept documents through personal email or messaging apps as the "official" copy, never save documents to your own device, and never accept a document you believe has been changed. Escalate.

</div>
