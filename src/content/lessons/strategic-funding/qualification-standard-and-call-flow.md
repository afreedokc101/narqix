## The Narqix qualification standard

A qualified merchant is one where:

1. We know **who** they are (legal business, owners, state, industry).
2. We know **what** they need and **why** (amount and use of funds).
3. We know their **cash flow** in their own words (deposits or revenue, time in business).
4. We know what they **already owe** (advances, loans, cards).
5. We know any **blockers** (credit band, bankruptcy, liens, judgments).
6. Their facts **meet the screening minimums** for at least one product.
7. They **understand the next step** and have agreed to it.

If any of the seven is missing, the lead is not qualified yet. It is "contacted".

## The ten core questions

Ask these on every business funding call. Product-specific questions come after.

| # | Question (say it simply) | Why we ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is the legal name of the business, and do you use a different name with customers? | Must match bank statements and the application |
| 2 | Which state is the business in, and what does it do? | State rules and industry rules both matter |
| 3 | When did the business start? | Time in business is a screening minimum for most products |
| 4 | How much are you looking for, and what will it be used for? | Use of funds decides the product more than the amount |
| 5 | On average, how much comes into your business bank account each month? | Deposits drive most cash flow products |
| 6 | Are you paying any cash advances, loans or business cards right now? With whom, how much, and how often? | Existing positions are the most important fact for funders |
| 7 | Roughly where is your personal credit score? | Self-reported only. No credit pull without written authorization |
| 8 | Any open bankruptcy, tax liens or court judgments? | Possible auto-stop flags |
| 9 | When do you need the funds? | Shapes product choice and follow-up. Never promise a timeline |
| 10 | Who owns 20% or more of the business? | Every such owner may need to sign and provide ID |

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: ask question 6 in three small parts. "Are you paying any advances or business loans right now?" Pause. "How many?" Pause. "And roughly what are the payments, daily or weekly?" Merchants answer small questions honestly. They dodge big ones.

</div>

## The call flow

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 1: Open (30 seconds)</summary>

- Greet, say your name and the company name.
- Give the recording disclosure if your campaign requires it.
- Inbound: "I'm following up on the request you just sent us." Outbound: say why you are calling and ask if now is a good time.
- Confirm you are speaking with an owner or decision maker.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 2: Discover the need (1 to 2 minutes)</summary>

- Ask questions 4 and 9 first: what the money is for and when it is needed.
- Let them talk. Write their words.
- Reflect back: "So you want to buy a second truck before the busy season, and you need to move in the next few weeks. Did I get that right?"

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 3: Qualify (3 to 5 minutes)</summary>

- Ask questions 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10.
- Then ask the product-specific questions from the Product Guide.
- If an answer clearly fails a screening minimum, finish politely; do not push through the rest.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 4: Match and explain the next step (1 to 2 minutes)</summary>

- Name the product path in plain words: "Based on what you shared, this looks like a fit for our equipment financing programs."
- Explain the process, not the outcome: application, documents, review by funders, written offers if approved.
- Say what you cannot say: "I can't tell you a rate or an amount. Those come only from the funder, in writing."

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 5: Application and documents (2 to 3 minutes)</summary>

- Send the application link and the secure upload link through a consented channel while on the call.
- Confirm they received them.
- Name the exact documents for their product.
- Agree a time for the documents and a time for your follow-up.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Stage 6: Close the call and record</summary>

- Thank them. Tell them who will contact them next and when.
- Disposition the lead, fill every required field, write the handoff note, set the next task.

</details>

## Matching facts to products

Use this table to decide which path to suggest. The desk lead and Processor confirm.

| What you hear | Path to suggest |
| --- | --- |
| Steady card or bank deposits, 4 or more months, needs money quickly | Merchant Cash Advance or Working Capital Loan |
| Uneven or seasonal revenue, 6 or more months | Revenue Based Financing |
| Several open advances, payments hurting cash flow | MCA Bailout (and an honest conversation about total cost) |
| 1 or more years, profitable, clear purpose, decent credit | Term Loans, possibly SBA 7(a) |
| Recurring gaps, decent credit | Lines of Credit |
| Buying a specific machine or vehicle | Equipment Lending |
| Waiting 30 to 90 days on invoices from businesses or government | Invoice Factoring |
| Large confirmed order, cannot pay the supplier | Purchase Order Factoring |
| Strong personal credit, startup or pre-revenue | 0% Business Credit Cards (with full card disclosures) |
| Low credit, declined before | Credit Repair, then re-check |
| Buying a business with a signed LOI | Mergers & Acquisitions |
| Investor buying a house to renovate and sell | Fix and Flip |
| Investor buying or refinancing a rental | DSCR Loans |
| Buying a home to live in | Residential Mortgages (licensed team only) |
| Commercial building, 500K or more | Commercial Real Estate |
| Building from land | Ground Up Construction |

## Recording answers

- Write numbers the way the merchant said them. "Said 30 to 35K a month" is better than "35K".
- Mark self-reported facts as self-reported. The scrub will check them.
- Never fill a field with a guess. Leave it blank and add a task to ask.
- Use the merchant's words for the use of funds.
- Write the consent status and preferred channel every time.

## When to stop the call

End politely and route correctly when:

- The merchant clearly fails the screening minimums for every product (use the Decline Handling script)
- The person is not an owner and cannot connect you with one
- The merchant asks you to remove them (honor it immediately)
- The merchant asks you to help hide or change anything (decline, note it, escalate)
- The merchant becomes abusive (end the call politely, note it, tell your lead)

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never pull or ask for a credit report without written authorization. Never tell a merchant they are approved, pre-approved or "sure to qualify". Never suggest an answer ("just say you've been open 6 months"). If the true answer does not qualify, the true answer goes in the file.

</div>
