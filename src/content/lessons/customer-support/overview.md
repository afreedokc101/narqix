For every Narqix specialist who answers a client's customers by email, chat, social or phone, and for the people who escalate and review that work. After this track you will handle tickets the Narqix way: accurate, warm, fast and fully owned.

Track: Customer Support  ·  Role: Support Agent, CSR  ·  Time to complete: 10 min (this page), about 7 hours (full track)

</div>

## What this track is

When you answer a customer, you **are** the client's business. The customer does not know or care that you sit in Dhaka or Manila. They care whether their problem is solved, whether you understood them, and whether you did what you promised.

Great support is not about being nice. It is about four things done every time:

1. **Read everything** before you reply.
2. **Answer everything** the customer asked, starting with what matters most to them.
3. **Own the next step**: who does what, by when.
4. **Keep every promise**, even when the news is "no news yet".

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: customers forgive a slow answer more easily than a wrong one, and forgive a "no" more easily than being ignored. Accuracy and honesty beat speed. Speed comes with practice.

</div>

6. **Chat and Phone Etiquette for US, UK and AU customers** (09).

## How this track fits with Narqix Standards

These universal rules live in the **Narqix Standards** section and apply to you every shift:

- **Data Security and Privacy**: verifying identity, card data, access, incidents.
- **Communication Standard**: plain English, time zones, client response times.
- **Escalation Ladder**: P1 to P4 and who to notify.
- **Reporting Standard**: start of day and end of day reports.
- **Attendance and Shift Standard**: your scheduled shift, staggered breaks so the queue is never empty.
- **AI Use Policy**: no customer personal data in unapproved tools; every AI-assisted reply fully read and edited by you.

This track teaches the craft on top of those rules.

## Who you represent

You represent the **client's** business. Use the client's approved greeting and sign-off. You never mention Narqix, your own country, other clients or pricing to customers. If a customer asks where you are, answer with the client's company and the client's market or office, following the client's approved wording. You never pretend to be someone else, and never claim an automated reply was written by a person or the other way round.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never type, store, repeat or ask for a full payment card number, CVV or password. If a customer sends one, follow the client's redaction process and tell them not to send it again.

</div>

## Tools you will use (generic names)

Ticketing system, live chat tool, phone system or softphone, order management or store admin, CRM, knowledge base, macros (saved replies), password manager, team channel, task tracker, and your campaign's time tracker. Each client's exact setup is in their Client Workflow Record.
