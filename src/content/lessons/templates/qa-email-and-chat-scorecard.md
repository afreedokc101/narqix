For QA Specialists, QA Reviewers, Campaign Leads and anyone who writes to customers. After reading this page you will be able to score a written reply (email, ticket or text) and a chat on the Narqix scorecards, and check your own message before you send it.

Track: All tracks  ·  Role: QA Specialist, QA Reviewer, Support Agent, Back Office  ·  Time to complete: 20 min

## Where this comes from

The criteria and pass marks are set on **Quality Standard and QA Scoring** in Narqix Standards. The Customer Support track uses the same weights with support wording on the **Support QA Scorecard** page. Both names are shown below, so the sheets match whichever page your team trains on.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Written replies (email, tickets, texts) | Pass **88%** |
| Complaints and sensitive replies (any channel) | Pass **90%** |
| Chat | Pass **85%** |
| New team member | 100% of the first 20 customer-facing messages reviewed, often before sending |
| Disputes | Within 3 working days, to your Campaign Lead |

## Written reply scorecard (pass 88%)

| Criterion (Standards name) | Support QA Scorecard name | Weight | Full marks | Half marks | Zero |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Every question answered; issue resolved or next step owned | Complete resolution | 25 | Every question answered; solved or clearly owned next step | One minor question missed, or next step vague | Main issue not addressed |
| Accuracy | Accuracy | 20 | Policy, prices, dates and order or file data all correct and checked | One minor detail wrong, no customer impact | Wrong information that affects the customer |
| Tone and personal touch | Empathy and personal touch | 15 | Personal first line proves the message was read; empathy sized to the problem | Generic but polite | Cold, robotic or bare saved reply |
| Structure and clarity | Clarity and structure | 10 | Answer first; short paragraphs; numbered steps | Answer buried or long paragraphs | Hard to understand |
| Grammar, spelling, correct names, no unfilled placeholders | Writing mechanics | 10 | No errors; name right; no blank fields | 1 to 2 small errors | Wrong name, blank field or many errors |
| Next step: who does what, by when | Ownership of next steps | 10 | Who, what and when, with time zone | Next step without a time | No next step when one is needed |
| Process: tags, status, notes, verification | Process and documentation | 10 | Correct status, tags, priority and internal note | One process item missing | Wrong status or no note on a handover |

## Chat scorecard (pass 85%)

| Criterion | Weight | Full marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greeting within 30 seconds, using their name and issue | 10 | Fast, specific greeting |
| Pace: no silent gaps, check-in times stated and kept | 15 | No gap longer than promised; holding messages used |
| Resolution | 25 | Solved, or an owned next step |
| Accuracy | 15 | All information correct |
| Short-form tone | 15 | Warm, clear, one idea per message |
| Saved replies personalised | 10 | Saved replies adapted to the conversation |
| Closing with outcome and next step | 10 | Outcome summarised, next step stated, "anything else" offered |

## Auto-fails (the item scores zero)

- [ ]  Data sent to the wrong person, or pasted into an unapproved tool
- [ ]  Required verification skipped before changing account details
- [ ]  False or misleading information (for example, saying a refund was sent when it was not)
- [ ]  Rude, sarcastic, dismissive or discriminatory language
- [ ]  Refund, credit or promise above authority without approval
- [ ]  A promise the writer knew would not be kept
- [ ]  Payment card data typed, stored or repeated
- [ ]  Compliance step missed (for example, a do-not-contact request ignored)

## Score yourself before you send

Run the five-point self-check from the Quality Standard on every message:

1. **Request:** does it answer exactly what was asked, every question?
2. **Source:** did I check the facts against the order, file or policy?
3. **Place:** right recipient, right ticket, right status?
4. **Record:** note, tags and status updated?
5. **Customer test:** would I be comfortable if the client read this?

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** The most common written deduction is a missed second question. Before you send, number the customer's questions on a scrap note and tick each one in your reply. It takes ten seconds and protects 25 points.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never send a message with an unfilled placeholder or the wrong customer name. Never paste customer data into an unapproved tool to "improve" a reply. Never promise a date the tracking or the policy does not support.

</div>
