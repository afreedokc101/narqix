<div class="callout callout-intro">

For lead managers, cold callers who run full discovery, and acquisitions. After reading this page you will be able to fill a complete HOMEKEY seller record that acquisitions can price from, without calling the seller back to repeat the first conversation.

Track: Real Estate  ·  Role: Lead Manager, Cold Caller, Acquisitions  ·  Time to complete: 15 min

</div>

## How to use this sheet

- Open it in the CRM before you dial. Fill the property facts from public records first.
- Run the call with the **Seller Discovery and Qualification (HOMEKEY)** script in the Script Library. The letters are a checklist, not an order. Most calls start with **M**.
- A lead is **not qualified** until every letter has an answer. "Not known" is an answer only when you asked and the seller did not know.
- Use the seller's own words for motivation and expectations, in quotation marks.
- Post the summary to acquisitions within 5 minutes of booking, and attach it to the calendar invite.

## The HOMEKEY letters

| Letter | Stands for | What you must learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H** | House | Condition, size, occupancy, big repairs |
| **O** | Owners | Everyone on title and everyone who helps decide |
| **M** | Motivation | The real reason, how long, and the cost of doing nothing |
| **E** | Encumbrances | Mortgage balance, payment status, back taxes, liens, HOA, code issues |
| **K** | Key dates | When they want or need to be done, and why |
| **E** | Expectations | Asking price, where it came from, what matters besides price |
| **Y** | Yes step | The agreed next step with a time and all decision makers |

## Lead grade

| Grade | HOMEKEY status | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hot** | All seven letters answered. Real motivation, timeline under 90 days | Offer call booked within 48 hours |
| **Warm** | Motivation real, but timeline over 90 days or a decision maker not on board | Follow-up in 7 to 30 days, code FU-WARM |
| **Nurture** | Price-driven only, or "maybe someday" | Follow-up in 90 days |
| **Dead** | Not the owner, sold, or no interest at all | Disposition and suppress as needed |

## Ready for acquisitions?

An appointment is ready only when every box is ticked.

- [ ]  Motivation is clear, in the seller's own words
- [ ]  Timeline is known
- [ ]  Condition is described well enough to pick a repair tier
- [ ]  The seller shared a price, or agreed to hear an offer
- [ ]  All decision makers will be on the call or at the walkthrough
- [ ]  Mortgage or liens are known, or the seller agreed to share them on the call
- [ ]  The seller confirmed the date and time, and knows who will call and why

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** The two most valuable lines on this sheet are "Real reason" and "Cost of doing nothing". Acquisitions builds the whole offer conversation around them. A sheet with perfect property facts and a thin motivation section produces a weak offer call.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never mark a lead Hot with a decision maker missing because the seller said "he'll be fine with it". Never fill a field with a guess; write "says about" or "not known". Never record Social Security numbers, bank details or full dates of birth.

</div>

## Check yourself

1. Which HOMEKEY letter do most calls start with?
2. A seller says the house is in "great shape". Which lines on the sheet help you check that?
3. What makes a lead Hot rather than Warm?
4. When must the summary reach acquisitions after you book the appointment?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. M, Motivation.
2. The H block: roof, HVAC, water heater, windows, plumbing, electrical, kitchen, baths, floors and paint, with ages.
3. All seven letters answered, real motivation and a timeline under 90 days. Warm has real motivation but a longer timeline or a decision maker not on board.
4. Within 5 minutes, posted to the acquisitions team and attached to the calendar invite.

</details>
