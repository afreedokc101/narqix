<div class="callout callout-intro">

For every Narqix team member. After reading this page you will be able to close any shift with an end-of-day report (EOD) and a handover that show real outcomes in numbers and let the next person start without asking you anything.

Track: All tracks  ·  Role: Everyone  ·  Time to complete: 15 min

</div>

## When and where

- **When:** before you check out, and only after the CRM, tracker and notes are updated. The EOD is built from the system, so the system must be complete first.
- **Where:** the place your campaign requires, at the same point in every shift.
- **Numbers:** pulled from the CRM, dialer or tracker. Never from memory. If a number is not available, write "not available" and why.
- **Source:** the rules are on the Reporting Standard page in Narqix Standards.

## Which version to use

| Your work | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Back office, support, specialist, data and list, funding desk (non-calling) | General version |
| Cold calling, texting, appointment setting, confirming, outbound qualification | Calling and texting version |
| Transaction coordination | General version plus the TC shift summary |
| Property preservation desk | Handover note and end-of-shift table on the Daily Desk SOP page |
| Any shift where work continues after you leave | Add the shift handover |

## General version

```
EOD (DATE)
Completed:
- (OUTCOME WITH NUMBER)
- (OUTCOME WITH NUMBER)
In progress:
- (TASK): (PROGRESS, e.g. 30 of 50), ETA (DATE, TIME AND ZONE)
Need from you:
- (SPECIFIC DECISION OR APPROVAL)
Flags:
- (RISKS, COMPLAINTS, UNUSUAL ITEMS)
Tomorrow's top 3:
1. (TASK)
2. (TASK)
3. (TASK)
Hours tracked: (X.X)
```

## Calling and texting version

```
EOD (DATE), (CAMPAIGN)
Dials: (NUMBER)    Live contacts: (NUMBER)    Conversations: (NUMBER)
Qualified leads: (NUMBER)    Appointments set: (NUMBER)
Callbacks scheduled: (NUMBER)
Texts sent: (NUMBER)    Replies handled: (NUMBER)
Do-not-call requests captured: (NUMBER), all coded same shift: Yes or No
Hot leads handed off: (LEAD ID OR NAME INITIALS, TO WHOM, TIME AND ZONE)
List notes: (WRONG NUMBER OR DISCONNECT RATE, DATA ISSUES)
Flags: (COMPLAINTS, LEGAL LANGUAGE, ANYTHING UNUSUAL)
Hours tracked: (X.X)
```

## TC shift summary

Transaction Coordinators add this after the general EOD. It comes from the Tracker and CRM Standards page in the Transaction Coordination track.

```
TC SHIFT SUMMARY: (DATE)
Closings next 3 business days: (ADDRESS, DATE, STATUS)
Red files: (ADDRESS, ONE LINE)
Yellow files: (ADDRESS, ONE LINE)
Wins today: (DEPOSITS RECEIVED, BUYERS LOCKED, ITEMS CLEARED, CLOSINGS FUNDED)
Tomorrow's first actions: (TOP 3)
```

## Shift handover

Add a handover any time work continues after you leave: a teammate on the next shift, a backup, or your own next shift after a day off. It is the Communication Standard handover format.

```
Handover for (CAMPAIGN):
Open items: (ITEM, STATUS, WHAT IS NEXT)
Promised to customers: (WHO, WHAT, BY WHEN)
Watch for: (ANYTHING UNUSUAL)
```

Property preservation desks use their own handover note and end-of-shift metrics table. Both are on the Daily Desk SOP page in the Property Preservation track.

## Day 1 version for trainees

New team members post this short version on their first training day only. From Day 2, use the real format.

```
EOD (DATE), Day 1
Completed: (WHAT YOU FINISHED)
Questions: (YOUR QUESTIONS, or "none")
Tomorrow: (WHAT YOU WILL STUDY OR DO)
```

## Rules for a strong EOD

- **Outcomes, not activity.** "Merged 14 duplicates, 2 held for review" beats "CRM work".
- **Every "In progress" item has an ETA** with date, time and zone.
- **Every promise to a customer** appears in the EOD or handover with its time.
- **Flags are patterns and risks.** Three customers asking the same thing is a flag. One rude caller who was handled well is not.
- **Under two minutes to read.** Put long lists in a linked sheet with a one-line summary.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Your client reads the "Need from you" and "Flags" lines first. Write them as if the client will read nothing else. A clear decision request with your recommendation often gets a one-word reply before you are back on shift.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never invent, round up or copy yesterday's numbers. Never report a task as complete if any part is still open. Never post the EOD before the CRM is updated. A false report is an honesty breach, not a reporting mistake.

</div>

## Before you post

- [ ]  CRM, tracker and notes updated first
- [ ]  Every number taken from the system, not memory
- [ ]  Times in the client's time zone with the zone named
- [ ]  "Need from you" is specific and has a deadline
- [ ]  Every promise to a customer listed with its time
- [ ]  Lead IDs or initials only, no full customer personal data
- [ ]  Readable in under 2 minutes

## Check yourself

1. What must be finished before you write the EOD?
2. Your dialer report will not load at check-out. What do you write in the Dials line?
3. Which version does a Transaction Coordinator post?
4. When do you add a shift handover?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. The CRM, tracker and notes must be updated, because the EOD numbers come from them.
2. "Not available, dialer report not loading at (TIME AND ZONE); will add tomorrow." Never guess.
3. The general version plus the TC shift summary.
4. Whenever work continues after you leave: a next-shift teammate, a backup, or your own next shift after time off.

</details>
