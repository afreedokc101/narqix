For dispositions managers and anyone building a cash buyer list. After reading this page you will be able to record a complete buyer profile, with every buy box field, contact consent and a buyer rating, the same day you speak with the buyer.

Track: Real Estate  ·  Role: Dispositions  ·  Time to complete: 15 min

</div>

## How to use this form

- Fill it during the **Buyer Qualification Call** in the Script Library. Type straight into the CRM buyer profile.
- Write **zip codes, not just city names.** "Anywhere in the county" is not a buy box.
- Record consent for email and text separately, with date and time. No consent, no texts.
- Rate the buyer A, B or C before you close the record. Set the re-qualify date 90 days out.

## The template

## Buyer rating

| Rating | Meaning | How you treat them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A buyer** | Proof of funds on file, closed with your client before or has a strong recent record, clear buy box, closes in 14 days or less | First call on every matching deal |
| **B buyer** | Active, clear buy box, but no proof of funds yet or no closing history with your client | Included in matching blasts, asked for proof of funds again |
| **C buyer** | New investor, unclear funding, vague buy box | Email blasts only, re-qualify in 90 days |

A buyer is never rated A until proof of funds is on file. Mark the profile "POF pending" until it arrives.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** The last question on the call, "what would you need to decide fast?", is the most valuable line on the profile. Use the buyer's own answer to build every deal message you send them.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never text a buyer without recorded consent. Never write "buys anywhere, any price" as a buy box. Never store bank statements in the CRM note; save proof of funds only where your client's process says, and note the date.

</div>

## Check yourself

1. Name five buy box fields.
2. What is the difference between an A buyer and a B buyer?
3. A buyer says "send me everything". What do you record?
4. When is the re-qualify date set?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Any five of: strategy, areas and zip codes, property types and minimums, price range, ARV range, rehab tiers accepted, deal-breakers, funding, proof of funds, closing speed, volume, entity name.
2. An A buyer has proof of funds on file and a strong closing record; a B buyer has a clear buy box but no proof of funds yet or no history with your client.
3. Finish the buy box questions first, so you only send matching deals. Record the real answers, never "anywhere, any price".
4. On the day of the call, 90 days out.

</details>
