<div class="callout callout-intro">

For QA Specialists, Campaign Leads and every caller who self-scores. After reading this page you will be able to score any outbound or inbound call on the Narqix call scorecard, check the auto-fails, and write coaching notes the caller can act on.

Track: All tracks  ·  Role: QA Specialist, Campaign Lead, All Callers  ·  Time to complete: 20 min

</div>

## Where this comes from

The criteria, weights and pass marks are set on **Quality Standard and QA Scoring** in Narqix Standards. Customer Support phone calls use the phone scorecard on the **Support QA Scorecard** page (copy below). This page is the scoring sheet.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Pass mark | **85%** |
| Below 70% | Coaching within 3 working days |
| Sample size | At least 5 items per week; 10 per week in the first 30 days on a campaign |
| Always reviewed | Every escalation, every complaint, every negative customer rating |
| Disputes | Within 3 working days, to your Campaign Lead |

## The call criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Full marks | Half marks | Zero |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening and disclosures** | 10 | Name, company, purpose and recording disclosure, as scripted | One element missing or out of order (not the recording disclosure) | Opening skipped or purpose unclear |
| **Listening and discovery** | 20 | Asked the right questions, let the person finish, followed up on what they said | Questions asked, but key follow-ups missed or interruptions | Script read at the person; answers ignored |
| **Accuracy** | 15 | Correct information about the client's offer, process and next steps | Minor slip corrected on the call | Wrong information that affects the customer |
| **Tone and clarity** | 15 | Calm, warm, clear pace, easy to understand | Understandable, but rushed, flat or hard to follow in places | Rude, confusing or impatient |
| **Call control and next step** | 15 | Kept the call on track, agreed a clear next step with a time | Next step agreed without a time, or call drifted | No next step when one was needed |
| **CRM notes and disposition** | 15 | Correct code, notes in the Narqix format, entered right after the call | Correct code, but note incomplete or late | Wrong code or no note |
| **Script and process** | 10 | Approved flow, approved answers to objections | Small deviations that did not change meaning | Unapproved claims or skipped required steps |

Half marks help reviewers stay consistent. QA Specialists calibrate on shared samples once a month.

## Auto-fails (the call scores zero)

- [ ]  Customer or client data shared with the wrong person, or put into an unapproved tool
- [ ]  Required verification step skipped
- [ ]  False or misleading information given
- [ ]  Rude, dismissive or discriminatory language
- [ ]  Missed compliance step: recording disclosure not given, a stop or do-not-call request not honoured, a call outside permitted hours
- [ ]  Refund, credit or promise above authority without approval
- [ ]  A promise the caller knew would not be kept

<details class="toggle"><summary>Worked example: real estate cold call</summary>

```
CALL QA · (CAMPAIGN) · reviewed by (REVIEWER NAME) · Oct 21, 2026
Caller: (CALLER NAME)    Call ID or recording: (CALL ID)
Call date and time: Oct 20, 2026, 6:12 PM CT    Type: OUTBOUND
Script used: Cold Call Opener    Disposition entered: FU-WARM

AUTO-FAIL CHECK: NONE

SCORES (full, half or zero)
Opening and disclosures (10): 10    Evidence: 0:04 name, company, purpose, recording notice
Listening and discovery (20): 10    Evidence: 1:40 owner said "my tenant leaves in March"; no follow-up on lease or rent
Accuracy (15): 15    Evidence: correct explanation of as-is purchase at 2:30
Tone and clarity (15): 15    Evidence: calm pace, short lines, owner relaxed by 1:00
Call control and next step (15): 7.5    Evidence: 3:55 "I'll call you later"; no day or time agreed
CRM notes and disposition (15): 15    Evidence: complete, entered 6:17 PM CT
Script and process (10): 10    Evidence: approved flow and objection answer

TOTAL: 82.5 out of 100    Result: BELOW PASS

KEEP DOING: 0:40 warm pause after "is this a bad time?" got the owner talking
FIX FIRST: 3:55 lock a callback with a day and time in the owner's zone
Better version: "Would early February work to check in, maybe a Tuesday evening around 6 your time?"
Coaching session: BOOKED FOR Oct 22, 2026
```

At 82.5 the call is below the 85% pass mark, so coaching is booked. Below 70% would require coaching within 3 working days.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Customer Support phone scorecard (pass 85%)</summary>

| Criterion | Weight | Full marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening and compliance | 10 | Approved greeting; recording disclosure if required |
| Verification | 10 | Client's identity check completed before account details |
| Listening and tell-back | 15 | No interrupting; problem restated and confirmed |
| Resolution | 25 | Solved, or an owned next step |
| Accuracy | 15 | All information correct |
| Holds and transfers | 10 | Permission, reason, time, check-ins; warm transfer |
| Clarity and tone | 10 | Calm pace, clear numbers, read-backs |
| Close and log | 5 | Summary, next step, logged within 5 minutes |

Complaints and escalations on any channel pass at **90%**.

</details>

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Every score needs a timestamp. "Discovery weak" teaches nothing. "1:40, owner mentioned the tenant leaving in March, no follow-up" shows the caller the exact moment to replay and fix. One "keep doing" and one "fix first" per call is enough to change behaviour.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never score a call you did not listen to in full. Never change a score because the caller is a top performer or a friend. Never coach a customer to give a good rating, and never re-do or hide an item to avoid review.

</div>

## Check yourself

1. What is the pass mark for calls, and what happens below 70%?
2. A caller gives a perfect call but does not honour a stop request at the end. What is the score?
3. What two coaching lines does every scored call get?
4. Within how long can a caller dispute a score?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 85%. Below 70% means coaching within 3 working days.
2. Zero. Not honouring a stop request is an auto-fail.
3. One "keep doing" and one "fix first", each with a timestamp, plus the better version.
4. Within 3 working days, to their Campaign Lead.

</details>
