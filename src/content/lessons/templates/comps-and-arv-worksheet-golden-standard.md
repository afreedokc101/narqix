## How to use this worksheet

- The method is taught on **Underwriting 101: ARV and the Narqix Golden Standard** in the Real Estate track. This page is the form you fill.
- Start inside the tight comp box. Widen **one rule at a time,** only when you cannot find 3 good comps, and write down every rule you widened.
- Use renovated sold comps only. As-is sales sense-check current value. Active and pending listings check the ARV. Neither is ARV evidence.
- Round the ARV **down** to a clean number (for example the nearest 5,000).
- Every ARV carries a confidence rating. Low confidence means the client reviews before any offer.

## The Golden Standard comp box

| Rule | Start with | Widen up to (maximum) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sold date | Last 3 to 6 months | 12 months (note the market trend) |
| Distance | 0.5 mile (0.25 mile in dense city areas) | 1 mile in suburbs; up to 2 to 3 miles in rural areas |
| Boundaries | Same neighborhood, same side of major roads, highways, rail lines, rivers; same school district | Never cross a boundary that clearly changes value |
| Property type and style | Same | Similar style only if no match exists |
| Year built | Within 5 years | Within 10 to 15 years |
| Living area | Within 10% to 15% of the subject | Within 20% |
| Bedrooms | Same count | Plus or minus 1, with an adjustment |
| Bathrooms | Same count | Plus or minus 1, with an adjustment |
| Condition | Renovated, similar finish to the planned rehab | Never use as-is sales for ARV |
| Sale type | Normal, arm's-length sale | Exclude family transfers, foreclosure auctions, unusual sales |

## Confidence rating

| Confidence | When |
| --- | --- |
| **High** | 3 or more renovated comps inside the tight box, consistent PPSF, actives agree |
| **Medium** | You widened one or two rules, or PPSF varies by more than 10% |
| **Low** | Few or no renovated comps, rural or unusual property, or actives disagree |

**Coach's note:** Fill section 5 every time, even when it feels like extra work. Reviewers and buyers trust an ARV much more when they can see which tempting sales you left out and why.

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never use an automated online value estimate as the ARV. Never average every sale in the area. Never use a comp across a highway or in a different school district because the number looks good. Never adjust with amounts your client has not set; flag the difference instead.

## Check yourself

1. What are the starting distance and sold-date limits in the comp box?
2. Comps sold for 210,000 at 1,200 sqft, 228,000 at 1,300 sqft and 222,000 at 1,250 sqft. The subject is 1,280 sqft. What is the ARV, rounded down to the nearest 5,000?
3. You widened the distance to 1 mile. What do you write, and which confidence rating is most likely?
4. Your ARV is above every similar active listing. What does that tell you?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 0.5 mile (0.25 in dense city areas) and sold in the last 3 to 6 months.
2. PPSF 175.00, 175.38 and 177.60; average about 176.00. ARV = 1,280 × 176.00 = 225,280, rounded down to 225,000.
3. Write the rule, the new limit and the reason in section 3. Medium, because a rule was widened.
4. It is probably too high. Re-check comps and adjustments.

</details>
