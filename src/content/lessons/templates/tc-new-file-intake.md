<div class="callout callout-intro">

For Transaction Coordinators. After reading this page you will be able to complete Day 0 intake on any new contract within 2 business hours: check the paperwork, build the folder, log every date and amount, and get the file ready to open with title.

Track: Transaction Coordination  ·  Role: Transaction Coordinator  ·  Time to complete: 20 min

</div>

## When to use it

- **Trigger:** acquisitions hands you a signed contract (same day as signing).
- **Deadline:** intake complete within **2 business hours.** Title opened within 4 business hours.
- **Status:** the file is NEW until intake is complete and the opening email is sent. Then it moves to TITLE-OPEN.
- **Method:** Stage 1 of the Closing Timeline page and the Tracker and CRM Standards page in the Transaction Coordination track.

## The intake form

Paste into the CRM file record. Every line gets a value, "none" or "not applicable".

```

Status: NEW    Heat: (GREEN, YELLOW OR RED)    Flags: (LIST OR NONE)

Acquisitions owner: (NAME)    Dispositions owner: (NAME OR NOT APPLICABLE)
```

## Intake checklist

- [ ]  Contract signed and dated by every seller and by the buyer entity (count lines, count signatures)
- [ ]  Every page that needs initials has them
- [ ]  All addenda and counteroffers signed by every party; final version clear
- [ ]  State wholesaler disclosure signed, dated and delivered on time (or client asked if unsure)
- [ ]  Seller cancellation window end date and time logged; no deed or closing document signed inside it
- [ ]  Seller names match the owner of record
- [ ]  Buyer entity name exact, including LLC or Inc. and any "and/or assigns" wording
- [ ]  Price, deposit, inspection end, closing, possession, closing costs, credits and personal property logged in the tracker
- [ ]  Deal type tag set
- [ ]  Occupancy and access logged
- [ ]  Known liens, back taxes, probate or divorce notes copied into the file
- [ ]  Every deadline on the calendar with a reminder 2 business days before

## Folder structure

One folder per property in the shared drive.

```
(PROPERTY ADDRESS) - (CITY) (STATE)
  01 Contract and Addenda
  02 Disclosures
  03 Title and Lien Searches
  04 Deposits and Receipts
  05 Buyer and Assignment
  06 Inspections and Photos
  07 Settlement Statements
  08 Closing Package
  09 Correspondence Exports
```

**File names:** `YYYY-MM-DD_DocType_StreetNumber-StreetName_Status.pdf`. No spaces. EXECUTED only when every party has signed. Approved document type words are listed on the Tracker and CRM Standards page.

## Opening email subject line

```
(PROPERTY ADDRESS) · New File · (DEAL TYPE) · Close (CLOSING DATE)
```

Send entity documents in a separate email from the contract.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Read the special terms box and every handwritten change twice. "Seller to leave the shed", "buyer pays all closing costs" or "closing contingent on seller finding housing" are the few words that cause most closing-week surprises. Copy them word for word into the intake form.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never open title on a file with a missing signature or a missing required state disclosure; escalate Red and let the client decide. Never choose a title company yourself. Never put Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, full dates of birth, ID copies or lockbox codes in CRM notes.

</div>

## Check yourself

1. How soon after receiving a signed contract must intake be complete?
2. The seller name on the contract differs from the owner of record. What do you do?
3. The state requires a wholesaler disclosure and it is not in the file. What happens?
4. Name the folder where the deposit receipt goes.

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Within 2 business hours.
2. Record "match: No" and follow the vesting and signing guidance (Title Issues and Fixes, Contract Titling and Signing). Raise it with the client before opening title.
3. Escalate Red before opening title. The fix is the client's decision.
4. 04 Deposits and Receipts.

</details>
