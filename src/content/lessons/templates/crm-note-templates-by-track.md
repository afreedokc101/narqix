For everyone who writes in a CRM, ticketing system or work order platform. After reading this page you will have the exact note format for your track, ready to copy, so anyone can pick up any record and continue without asking you a question.

Track: All tracks  ·  Role: Everyone  ·  Time to complete: 25 min

</div>

## Three rules for every note

1. **Write it within 5 minutes,** before the next call or task. On calling campaigns: before the next dial.
2. **Facts, numbers and their words.** No opinions about people. Quote short phrases when they matter.
3. **Every open record ends with a next step:** action, owner, date, time and zone.

The full rules are on CRM Hygiene and Note Writing in Core Skills. Track pages add the codes for your campaign.

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never put passwords, full card or bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, full dates of birth or copies of IDs in any note. Never write insults, jokes or opinions about a customer. Never edit or delete another person's note.

</div>

## The universal Narqix note

Use this on every track when no track-specific format exists. Remember it as **Who, What, Details, Promised, Next.**

```
(DATE) (TIME AND ZONE) · (CHANNEL) · (OUTCOME CODE)
Who: (NAME AND ROLE OF THE PERSON YOU SPOKE WITH)
What happened: (2 TO 3 SHORT SENTENCES OF FACTS)
Key details: (NUMBERS, DATES, CONDITIONS, DECISIONS)
Promised: (ANYTHING YOU OR THEY PROMISED, WITH TIMES)
Next step: (ACTION) by (OWNER) on (DATE, TIME AND ZONE)
```

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: support ticket</summary>

```
Oct 19, 2026 2:40 PM ET · Chat · Pending customer
Who: Customer, order 51877
What happened: Customer reports the blender arrived without its lid. Sent 2 photos showing the open box.
Key details: Order paid USD 240, delivered Oct 17. Inside 30-day window. Replacement part in stock.
Promised: Replacement lid shipped within 1 business day; tracking emailed when it ships.
Next step: Warehouse ticket raised by me; I confirm tracking to customer by Oct 20, 5:00 PM ET.
```

</details>

## The universal handoff note

Add this whenever you pass a lead, ticket, file or task to another person.

```
HANDOFF to (ROLE OR NAME) · (DATE) (TIME AND ZONE)
Why now: (WHY THIS IS BEING HANDED OFF)
Situation in one line: (SUMMARY)
What they want: (THE CUSTOMER'S GOAL)
Watch out for: (SENSITIVITIES, OBJECTIONS, DEADLINES)
Promised: (WHAT AND WHEN)
```

A handoff is complete only when three things are true: the CRM stage is updated, the note is written, and the receiving person is notified the way your campaign uses (CRM task, team channel, or both).

## Real Estate

<details class="toggle"><summary>Cold call note (Cold Caller, SMS Agent)</summary>

One disposition code per call, from the Narqix standard set: NA / VM, BUSY / DISC, WRONG, DNC, NI, CB, FU-WARM, HOT, APPT, LISTED / AGENT, SOLD, LIT-FLAG.

```
CODE: (DISPOSITION CODE)
Owner confirmed: (YES OR NO), names on title if known
Motivation (their words): "(THEIR WORDS)"
Occupancy: (OWNER, TENANT WITH LEASE END AND RENT, OR VACANT)
Condition: (NUMBER) out of 10. To make it a 10: (THEIR ANSWER)
Timeline: (THEIR ANSWER)
Price mentioned: (AMOUNT, or "none given")
Decision makers: (NAMES, AND WHETHER THEY JOINED)
Text consent: (YES OR NO), (NUMBER), (DATE, TIME AND ZONE)
Next step: (WHAT), (WHO), (WHEN, OWNER'S LOCAL TIME)
```

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Standard real estate handoff note (Cold Caller or SMS Agent to Lead Manager or Acquisitions)</summary>

Write it in this order every time. Short phrases are fine.

```
Lead: (SELLER NAME), (PROPERTY ADDRESS)
Temperature and code: (HOT, APPT OR FU-WARM)
Motivation: (THE REASON, IN THE SELLER'S WORDS)
Timeline: (WHEN THEY WANT OR NEED TO SELL)
Condition: (ROOF AGE, HVAC AGE, KITCHEN AND BATHS, KNOWN PROBLEMS), suggested tier: (EASY, NEEDS WORK, FULL COSMETIC OR FULL GUT)
Price: (THEIR NUMBER, AND HOW THEY ARRIVED AT IT)
Money owed: (MORTGAGE, LIENS, BACK TAXES, IF KNOWN)
Occupancy and listing: (OWNER, TENANT OR VACANT), (LISTED OR NOT)
Decision makers: (EVERYONE ON THE DEED OR WHO MUST AGREE)
Best contact: (PHONE, EMAIL, BEST TIME IN THEIR ZONE), texts agreed: (YES OR NO)
Promised next step: (EXACTLY WHAT YOU TOLD THE SELLER, AND WHEN)
```

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Acquisitions brief (Lead Manager to Acquisitions)</summary>

Send it with the calendar invite, at least 2 hours before the appointment. It is the standard handoff note above plus these lines, and the full HOMEKEY record from the Seller Intake Sheet page.

```
Seller read: (COMMUNICATION STYLE: DIRECT, SOCIAL, STEADY OR ANALYTICAL), (EMOTIONAL STATE), (WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT MOST)
Objections already heard: (OBJECTION), seller reaction: (THEIR WORDS)
Promised: (ANYTHING YOU PROMISED, WITH TIMES)
Attached: (COMPS OR PROPERTY DETAILS THE CLIENT ASKS FOR)
Appointment: (DAY, DATE, TIME AND ZONE), attendees: (ALL DECISION MAKERS)
```

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Buyer call log (Dispositions, deal presentation)</summary>

```
Deal: (PROPERTY ADDRESS), deal ID (DEAL ID)
Buyer: (BUYER NAME), rating (A, B OR C)
Result: (WALKTHROUGH BOOKED, OFFER RECEIVED, THINKING WITH CALLBACK SET, OR PASSED)
```

## Transaction Coordination

<details class="toggle"><summary>Daily TC file note</summary>

Every active file gets a dated note every business day it is active. It answers: what happened, what we are waiting for, what is next, who owns it, and how risky it is.

```
(DATE) · Status: (CODE) · Heat: (GREEN, YELLOW OR RED)
Done: (WHAT HAPPENED TODAY, WITH TIMES FOR CALLS)
Waiting on: (WHO, WHAT, SINCE WHEN)
Next: (ACTION) by (DATE), owner (NAME OR ROLE)
Risk: (NONE, or one line)
```

Status codes: NEW, TITLE-OPEN, EMD-PENDING, SEARCH, CURATIVE, MARKETING, BUYER-LOCKED, CTC, SS-REVIEW, SIGNING, FUNDING, POST, CLOSED, HOLD, CANCEL-PENDING, DEAD. Flags and dead reason codes are on the Tracker and CRM Standards page.

For any important call (seller worries, cancellation talk, price questions), add the speaker's key words in quotation marks.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: TC file note</summary>

```
2026-10-20 · Status: SEARCH · Heat: Green
Done: Called title closer 11:05 a.m. CT. Commitment expected 10/22. Seller returned the seller information sheet and payoff authorization 10/19.
Waiting on: Title commitment and municipal lien search (ordered 10/14).
Next: Read commitment the day it arrives and send title summary to client by 10/22, owner TC.
Risk: None.
```

</details>

## HVAC and Home Services

<details class="toggle"><summary>Booking note (CSR, Call Booker)</summary>

Times are in the customer's local time unless your client says otherwise.

```
(DATE) (TIME) local · (YOUR NAME) · Channel: (INBOUND CALL, OUTBOUND CALL, TEXT OR WEB)
Reason: (PROBLEM IN THE CUSTOMER'S WORDS, WHICH UNIT, SINCE WHEN)
Safety screen: (CLEAR, or WHAT WAS FOUND AND WHAT YOU DID), (VULNERABLE OCCUPANT IF ANY), (INSIDE AND OUTSIDE TEMPERATURE IF RELEVANT)
Equipment: (SYSTEM TYPE, BRAND IF KNOWN, AGE AS THE CUSTOMER ESTIMATES)
Outcome: Booked (DAY) (ARRIVAL WINDOW), tech (TECH NAME), priority (P1, P2, P3 OR P4). Diagnostic fee (FEE) disclosed and accepted: (YES OR NO)
Member: (YES OR NO). Maintenance plan mentioned: (RESPONSE)
Access: (GATE CODE, PETS, WHO WILL BE HOME)
Lead source: (SOURCE)
Next step: (CONFIRMATION TEXT, ON-THE-WAY TEXT, OR OTHER)
```

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Unbooked call note</summary>

Every call that does not book gets a reason code and a next date. Codes: NL-Existing, NL-Vendor, NL-Job, NL-Spam, NL-Short, NL-Internal (not a lead), and UB-Fee, UB-PhonePrice, UB-Timing, UB-Area, UB-Service, UB-Shopping, UB-Renter, UB-Fixed, UB-DIY, UB-Callback, UB-Other (lead, not booked).

```
(DATE) (TIME) local · (YOUR NAME) · Channel: (CHANNEL)
Reason for call: (THEIR WORDS)
Code: (NL OR UB CODE)
Their words on why not booked: "(THEIR WORDS)"
Offered: (WHAT YOU OFFERED, e.g. other slots, membership, cancellation list)
Next step: (FOLLOW-UP ACTION) on (DATE AND TIME), or none for NL codes
```

</details>

## Strategic Funding

<details class="toggle"><summary>Qualification note (Lead Qualifier)</summary>

Write numbers the way the merchant said them, and mark self-reported facts as self-reported.

```
QUAL · (DATE TIME) · (YOUR NAME)
Product path: (PRODUCT) · Reason: (ONE LINE)
Use of funds (their words): (TEXT) · Amount requested: (AMOUNT) · Timing: (TIMING)
Legal name: (NAME) · DBA: (DBA) · State: (STATE) · Industry: (INDUSTRY) · Start date: (DATE)
Deposits, self-reported: (AS SAID) · Positions, self-reported: (COUNT, AMOUNTS, FREQUENCY)
Credit band, self-reported: (BAND) · Bankruptcy, liens, judgments: (ANSWER) · Owners 20%+: (NAMES AND PERCENT)
Consent: call (Y/N), text (Y/N), email (Y/N) · App link sent via: (CHANNEL)
Flags: (ANY OR NONE)
Next: (ACTION) by (DATE TIME) · Owner: (TEAMMATE)
```

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Document notes (Application Assistant and Document Chaser)</summary>

```
DOCS REQUEST · (DATE TIME) · (YOUR NAME) · Channel: (CALL, EMAIL, TEXT)
Requested: (EXACT LIST)
Already received: (LIST OR NONE)
Merchant's promised time: (DATE TIME)
Next: check uploads at (DATE TIME), start missing-document sequence if needed
```

```
DOCS FOLLOW-UP · Touch (NUMBER) · (DATE TIME) · (YOUR NAME)
Channel: (CHANNEL) · Result: (REACHED, VOICEMAIL, NO ANSWER, REPLIED)
Still missing: (LIST)
```

Set the file to "Docs Pending" with a task at the promised time.

</details>

## Property Preservation

<details class="toggle"><summary>Order note (Work Order Processor, Photo QC, Bid Writer)</summary>

Record every change on an order with the time. Use the tracker status names: New, Accepted, Dispatched, In field, Photos received, QC hold, QC passed, Submitted, Rejected, Approved, Invoiced, Paid, Short paid.

```
(DATE) (TIME AND ZONE) · Order (ORDER NUMBER) · Status: (STATUS)
Property: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Client: (CLIENT NAME) · Due: (DUE DATE)
Tasks: (ORDERED TASKS)
What happened: (FACTS: CREW UPDATE, ACCESS PROBLEM, QC RESULT, CLIENT MESSAGE)
Photos: (RECEIVED, MISSING ITEMS, OR QC PASSED)
Bids: (NONE, or ITEM, AMOUNT, STATUS)
Client contact: (WHO, WHAT WAS ASKED OR ANSWERED, TIME)
Next: (ACTION) by (OWNER) on (DATE, TIME AND ZONE)
```

</details>

```
Oct 20, 2026 2:15 AM ET · Order (ORDER NUMBER) · Status: QC hold

Tasks: Grass re-cut, debris removal 6 cubic yards

Bids: None
Client contact: None yet
Next: Crew to return for rear yard after photos by Oct 20, 11:00 AM ET, owner Processor; extension request if not received by 9:00 AM ET
```

</details>

## Customer Support and Back Office

Use the universal Narqix note and the universal handoff note above. Ticket statuses, tags and priorities come from your Client Playbook and the Ticket Lifecycle page. Back office task logs use the universal note with "Internal" as the channel.

## Check yourself

1. What are the five lines of the universal note, after the header line?
2. What three things make a real estate handoff complete?
3. A homeowner on a solar campaign says "please stop calling me". Which note do you write?
4. Which TC status does a file carry while title is fixing a probate requirement?
5. On a funding file, how much of an account number may appear in a note?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Who, What happened, Key details, Promised, Next step.
2. CRM stage updated, handoff note written in the standard format, receiving person notified.
3. "DNC. Requested on call at (TIME). Removed and confirmed to homeowner." with the DNC code.
4. CURATIVE. Every active file has exactly one status. If the parties agree to pause the deal for probate, it becomes HOLD.
5. Only the last 4 digits.

</details>
