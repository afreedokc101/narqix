For acquisitions and lead managers who prepare offers. After reading this page you will be able to calculate the buyer's maximum, the MAO, the offer range and the rental check step by step, and record the numbers in a form the client can approve at a glance.

Track: Real Estate  ·  Role: Acquisitions, Lead Manager  ·  Time to complete: 20 min

</div>

## The order never changes

**Repairs, then ARV, then MAO, then the offer range.** The method is taught on the Making Offers page in the Real Estate track. Bring the numbers from the Repair Tier Worksheet and the Comps and ARV Worksheet.

## The formulas

| Step | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer's maximum | (ARV × percentage) − repairs |
| MAO | (ARV × percentage) − repairs − the client's fee |
| Opening offer | About 10% to 15% below the MAO |
| Target | About 5% to 7% below the MAO |
| Walk-away | The MAO. Not one dollar higher without client approval |
| Rental check (B and C class) | (Monthly market rent ÷ class rent factor) − repairs − the client's fee |

## Choosing the percentage

| Market and deal | Percentage |
| --- | --- |
| Normal market, normal rehab | **70%** |
| Slow market, heavy rehab, lower-value area, or low ARV confidence | **65%** |
| Fast market, high-value area, light rehab, strong buyer demand | **75%,** only with the client's approval |

**Class rent factor:** A = 0.005, B = 0.01, C = 0.025. Market rent is the average of 3 similar rentals nearby. For B and C class areas, use the **lower** of the MAO and the rental check result, unless the client says otherwise.

**Client's fee:** set by the client. If the client has not set one, Narqix plans with 10K to 15K and confirms with the client before any offer.

```

INPUTS
ARV: (ARV)    Confidence: (HIGH, MEDIUM OR LOW)    Class: (A, B OR C)

Client's fee: (AMOUNT), confirmed with client on (DATE)

STEP 1. BUYER'S MAXIMUM
(ARV) × (PERCENTAGE) = (RESULT A)
(RESULT A) − (REPAIRS) = (BUYER'S MAXIMUM)

STEP 2. MAO
(BUYER'S MAXIMUM) − (CLIENT'S FEE) = (MAO)

(MONTHLY RENT) ÷ (CLASS RENT FACTOR) = (TOTAL INVESTMENT)
(TOTAL INVESTMENT) − (REPAIRS) − (CLIENT'S FEE) = (RENTAL CHECK PRICE)
Lower of MAO and rental check: (WALK-AWAY NUMBER)

STEP 4. OFFER RANGE
Opening offer (10% to 15% below walk-away): (OPENING OFFER)
Target (5% to 7% below walk-away): (TARGET)
Walk-away: (WALK-AWAY NUMBER)

SELLER CONTEXT
Seller's asking price: (ASKING PRICE)
Gap between asking price and walk-away: (AMOUNT)
Options if cash does not reach: (RETAIL PARTNERSHIP OPTION, SELLER FINANCING, SUBJECT-TO, or NONE), client decides

APPROVALS
Client approval needed before offer: (YES OR NO)
Approved by: (CLIENT NAME) on (DATE, TIME AND ZONE)
Market Compliance Card checked for (STATE): (YES)
```

## Worked example: the 1,450 sqft ranch

ARV **250,000** (High confidence), Full Cosmetic repairs **50,000**, B-class, normal market, client's fee **15,000**, seller asking **165,000**.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Buyer's maximum | 250,000 × 70% = 175,000; 175,000 − 50,000 | **125,000** |
| 2. MAO | 125,000 − 15,000 | **110,000** |
| 3. Rental check | Rent average 1,800; 1,800 ÷ 0.01 = 180,000; 180,000 − 50,000 − 15,000 | 115,000 |
| Walk-away | Lower of 110,000 and 115,000 | **110,000** |
| 4. Opening offer | About 13% below 110,000 (95,700), rounded to a clean number | **95,000** |
| Target | About 6% below 110,000 (103,400), rounded to a clean number | **103,000** |

**What happened:** acquisitions explained the comps, opened at 95,000 and agreed at 103,000. Dispositions assigned the contract to a buyer at 118,000. The client's fee is 15,000, and the buyer is at 118,000, under the buyer's maximum of 125,000.

<details class="toggle"><summary>More practice</summary>

1. ARV 200,000, Needs Work repairs 40,000, fee 12,000, normal market. → 200,000 × 70% = 140,000; − 40,000 = 100,000; − 12,000 = **MAO 88,000.**
2. 900 sqft, ARV 140,000, Easy repairs 10,000, slow C-class market, fee 10,000. → 140,000 × 65% = 91,000; − 10,000 = 81,000; − 10,000 = **MAO 71,000.**
3. 2,200 sqft, ARV 380,000, Full Gut 100K to 120K with clear seller answers (use the middle, 110,000), fee 15,000. → 380,000 × 70% = 266,000; − 110,000 = 156,000; − 15,000 = **MAO 141,000.**
4. B-class rental check: rent 1,200, repairs 20,000, fee 10,000. → 1,200 ÷ 0.01 = 120,000; − 20,000 − 10,000 = **90,000.**

## Before you send any offer

- [ ]  ARV set with the Golden Standard, confidence rating noted
- [ ]  Repair tier and estimate noted, with any red flags
- [ ]  Percentage chosen and justified (65%, 70% or 75%)
- [ ]  Client's fee confirmed
- [ ]  MAO, opening offer and target calculated
- [ ]  Rental check done for B and C class areas
- [ ]  Client approval obtained if your campaign requires it
- [ ]  All decision makers included
- [ ]  Market Compliance Card ready for the state

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Show your work on the form, line by line. A client who can see 250,000 × 70% − 50,000 − 15,000 approves in one reply. A client who only sees "MAO 110,000" asks questions, and the seller waits.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never go above the walk-away number without written approval from the client. Never reduce the repair estimate or raise the ARV to make the numbers work. Never use 75% without the client's approval.

</div>

## Check yourself

1. Write the MAO formula.
2. When do you use 65% instead of 70%?
3. In a B-class area, the MAO is 92,000 and the rental check gives 86,000. What is the walk-away number?
4. The MAO is 120,000. What are the opening offer and target ranges?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. MAO = (ARV × percentage) − repairs − the client's fee.
2. Slow market, heavy rehab, lower-value area, or low ARV confidence.
3. 86,000, the lower of the two, unless the client says otherwise.
4. Opening about 102,000 to 108,000 (10% to 15% below). Target about 111,600 to 114,000 (5% to 7% below).

</details>
