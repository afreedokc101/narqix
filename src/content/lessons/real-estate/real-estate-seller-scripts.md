## What's in this section

Twelve seller-side scripts that cover the full journey, from the first cold call to a signed agreement and beyond. Each one is complete enough to use on a live call: stages, spoken lines, the reason each stage works, real branches for what sellers actually say, objections, and the exact CRM note to write afterward.

Objection handling, the closer guide and buyer-side dispositions scripts sit alongside these in the Script Library.

## The seller journey

| Step | What happens | Script |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First contact | Owner hears from us for the first time, or calls us | Cold Call Opener, Inbound Seller Call |
| Qualification | We understand the seller and the house | Seller Discovery and Qualification (HOMEKEY), Property Condition Walkthrough |
| Booking | The offer call is set with every decision maker | Appointment Setting for Acquisitions |
| Offer | The property is approved on the call and the offer is presented | The Offer Call, Retail Partnership Option |
| Agreement | The seller signs with full understanding | Contract Walkthrough Call |
| Under contract | New facts are handled with evidence | Price Re-Negotiation Call |
| Not yet | We stay in touch the right way | Seller Follow-Up Call |
| Every step | Written and recorded touches | Voicemail and Text Pack, Seller Email Pack |

## Learning path

Learn the scripts in this order. Each one builds on the last. Role-play each script at least three times with your team lead before using it live.

| Order | Script | Main role | Stage | You will learn to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cold Call Opener | Cold Caller | Opening | Open with confidence, confirm the owner, check interest, and hand off |
| 2 | Inbound Seller Call | Lead Manager, Cold Caller | Inbound | Turn a mailer or text callback into a qualified lead |
| 3 | Seller Discovery and Qualification (HOMEKEY) | Lead Manager | Qualification | Run a full discovery conversation with the HOMEKEY framework |
| 4 | Property Condition Walkthrough by Phone | Lead Manager, Acquisitions | Qualification | Score the house room by room and place it in a rehab tier |
| 5 | Appointment Setting for Acquisitions | Lead Manager, Appointment Setter | Qualification | Book offer calls that actually happen, with every decision maker |
| 6 | The Offer Call | Acquisitions | Offer & Close | Run the approval holds, deliver the approval moment, and move price in staged steps |
| 7 | Retail Partnership Option | Acquisitions | Offer & Close | Pivot to the open-market option when the last cash offer does not work |
| 8 | Contract Walkthrough Call | Acquisitions | Offer & Close | Explain the agreement in plain words and guide the e-signature |
| 9 | Price Re-Negotiation Call | Acquisitions | Offer & Close | Handle inspection and title findings with evidence and choices |
| 10 | Seller Follow-Up Call | Lead Manager, Acquisitions | Follow-Up | Re-engage sellers at 1 week, 30 days and 90 days |
| 11 | Seller Voicemail and Text Pack | All seller-facing roles | Voicemail & Text | Leave live voicemails and drops, send first-touch and follow-up texts, and handle every reply |
| 12 | Seller Email Pack | Lead Manager, Acquisitions | Email | Write offer, agreement and follow-up emails that match the call |

**Cold callers:** scripts 1, 2, 11 are required. Read 3 to understand what happens after your handoff.

**Lead managers and appointment setters:** scripts 1 to 5, 10, 11 and 12 are required.

**Acquisitions:** all twelve.

## Two Narqix tools you'll use everywhere

### HOMEKEY: the qualification checklist

| Letter | Stands for | What you must learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H | House | Condition, occupancy, big repairs |
| O | Owners | Everyone on title and every decision maker |
| M | Motivation | The real reason and the cost of doing nothing |
| E | Encumbrances | Mortgage, back taxes, liens, HOA, code issues |
| K | Key dates | When they want or need to be done |
| E | Expectations | Asking price, where it came from, what matters besides price |
| Y | Yes step | The agreed next step, with a time and all decision makers |

### Rehab tiers

| Tier | In short |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Move-in ready or close. Small fixes |
| Needs Work | Livable but tired. Paint, floors, some fixtures, maybe one system |
| Full Cosmetic | Everything visible needs updating. Structure fine |
| Full Gut | Down to the studs. Structural, fire, water, mold or several failed systems |

## Standards every script follows

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Identity and location**

Say your name and the company on every call.

When a seller asks where you are calling from, answer with the company and its market: "I'm with (COMPANY NAME). We buy houses all over (MARKET)." Follow the client's approved wording. Never mention your own country.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**How the close works**

Never negotiate price live. Price comes from the approver, during a hold, in small staged steps.

Get the seller's number and their "yes, if they come up" before every hold.

Send the agreement while the seller is watching the inbox, then walk them through it line by line.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Compliance**

Call and text only inside the calling window in the seller's local time (Narqix default: 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday, no Sundays or US holidays, with stricter state rules applied).

No more than 3 touches per seller per 24 hours, across calls, voicemails and texts.

Announce recording on every call.

Honor any stop request, in any words, immediately, and code DNC.

Texts, first-touch texts and prerecorded voicemail drops go out only through the client's registered platforms, on the lists your campaign has cleared for that channel.

Only the client's registered US numbers. No personal phones.

Check the state's Market Compliance Card before any offer or agreement.

</div>

## Disposition codes used in these scripts

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| NA / VM | No answer or voicemail |
| BUSY / DISC | Busy or disconnected |
| WRONG | Wrong number, tenant, or not the owner |
| DNC | Asked to stop, by any wording. Never contact again |
| NI | Not interested, did not ask to stop |
| CB | Callback requested at a set time |
| FU-WARM | Some motivation, no timeline yet |
| HOT | Motivation, timeline, condition and price discussed |
| APPT | Discovery or offer call booked |
| LISTED / AGENT | Listed with an agent, or the person is an agent |
| SOLD | Already sold |
| LIT-FLAG | Legal threats or lawsuit language. Escalate the same day |
| OFFER | Offer made, decision pending |
| NEG | Negotiating price or terms |
| RPO | Retail Partnership Option presented |
| UC | Agreement signed, under contract |

## How to practice

1. Read the script fully once, including every "If they say" toggle.
2. Say every spoken line out loud. Change words that feel unnatural for you, but keep the meaning and every compliance line.
3. Role-play with a teammate playing a calm seller, then a rushed seller, then an angry seller.
4. Record your practice inside your campaign's approved tools only, and review it with your team lead.
5. Pass the role-play check before your first live call on that script.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Scripts are a map, not a cage. The best callers know the script so well that they can leave it to follow the seller, then come back to it. That only happens with practice. Learn the stages and the "why it works" notes, not just the words.

</div>
