## What this role is

Many owners will not answer a call from an unknown number but will reply to a short, polite text. The SMS agent works the **conversation inbox**: every reply from an owner lands with you, and you decide, quickly and kindly, what happens next.

Texting is also the channel with the **strictest rules**. One mishandled opt-out can expose the client to legal claims. That is why Narqix SMS agents are trained to treat compliance as the first part of the job, not the last.

## What you own

- **Reply handling:** reading and answering every inbound reply during your shift.
- **Qualification by text:** gently learning the Four Pillars, one question at a time.
- **Conversion to a call:** moving interested owners to a phone conversation with a caller, Lead Manager or acquisitions.
- **Opt-out hygiene:** every stop request, in any words, coded DNC immediately.
- **Tagging and notes:** every conversation tagged and summarised in the CRM.

You do **not** own: deciding who receives texts, writing new campaign templates without approval, or sending texts outside the registered campaign.

## Texting rules you must know

These are summarised here. The full detail is in Calling Compliance for Real Estate.

- Texts go only through the **client's registered texting campaign** (registered under the client's business, not Narqix).
- Texts go only to **permitted recipients**: owners who gave consent, owners already in a conversation, or owners who agreed during a call to receive texts. Your campaign's rules decide this, not you.
- **First-touch texts to cold lists** use templates TXT C1 to C3 in the Seller Voicemail and Text Pack, on the lists your campaign has cleared for texting.
- **Identify the business** in the first message.
- Send only inside the owner's **permitted window** (Narqix default 9 AM to 8 PM local time, Monday to Saturday, no Sundays or US holidays; stricter state rules apply).
- **No more than 3 touches per owner in 24 hours** across calls and texts together.
- **Opt-out keywords:** STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE. The law also counts any reasonable way of saying stop, such as "remove me", "don't text me", "wrong number, leave me alone", or the same in Spanish or any language.
- US rules give a maximum of **10 business days** to honor an opt-out. **Narqix honors it immediately.**
- After an opt-out, send **at most one** short confirmation if your campaign uses one. Then nothing, ever, from any client campaign to that number.

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never reply to "STOP" with a sales message, a question, or "Are you sure?". Never text an owner who opted out from a different number or campaign. Never text outside the permitted window, even if the owner texted you first at night. Wait until the window opens.

</div>

## How a good text conversation sounds

Short. Human. One question at a time. No pressure.

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: owner replies &quot;Maybe. What are you offering?&quot;</summary>

**You:** Thanks for getting back to me. I'm (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We buy houses as-is. To give you a real number, our team looks at condition and the area first. Can I ask what's got you thinking about selling?

**Them:** Tenants moved out and left it a mess.

**You:** Sorry to hear that, that's stressful. Is it empty now?

**Them:** Yes.

**You:** Got it. Would a quick 5 minute call be easier to go over the details? I can have someone call you today or tomorrow, whatever suits you.

**Them:** Tomorrow after 5.

**You:** Perfect. Someone from our team will call you tomorrow after 5 PM your time. Thanks, (SELLER NAME).

*Why it works:* each message has one purpose, the agent shows empathy, and the next step is clear and agreed.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: owner replies &quot;Who is this? How did you get my number?&quot;</summary>

**You:** Fair question. I'm (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We contact owners of properties in your area using public property records. If you're not interested, just reply STOP and we won't message you again.

*Why it works:* honest, calm, and gives an easy way out.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: owner replies &quot;Wrong number&quot;</summary>

Code it **WRONG** and, because "wrong number" from an annoyed person is often a stop request, follow your campaign's rule. When in doubt, treat it as **DNC**. Do not reply with questions.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Example: owner replies with insults or threats</summary>

Do not reply. Code **DNC**. If the message mentions lawyers, lawsuits, regulators or your company's legal name, code **LIT-FLAG** and escalate to your team lead the same day.

</details>

## Your daily rhythm

| Block | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Start of shift | Check in using your campaign's check-in method, start your tracker. Clear overnight replies (read them now, reply only when each owner's window is open). |
| Priority replies | Interested replies first, then questions, then neutral replies. |
| Opt-out sweep | Confirm every stop request from overnight and today is coded DNC. |
| Conversation block | Work live conversations, aim to reply within minutes. |
| Handoffs | Book calls, send handoffs with notes. |
| End of shift | Every conversation tagged and noted. Report sent. Check out. |

## Quality checklist

- [ ]  Every opt-out coded DNC immediately, in any wording or language.
- [ ]  No texts outside the window.
- [ ]  Every first message identifies the business.
- [ ]  One question per message.
- [ ]  No prices, offers or promises by text.
- [ ]  Every interested owner given a clear next step.
- [ ]  Every conversation tagged and noted.

## Common mistakes (and the fix)

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Long messages full of selling points | One or two short sentences. One question. |
| Missing a free-text opt-out ("pls stop") | Read every reply. Keywords are not the only stop. |
| Replying at night because the owner texted at night | Wait for the window |
| Asking for the price first | Motivation first |
| Copy-paste replies that do not match the owner's message | Read, then reply to what they actually said |
| Slang, emojis, all caps | Clear, polite, professional |

## Escalation rules

- **Immediately:** legal threats, regulator mentions, anyone claiming they never consented and demanding records.
- **Same day:** carrier delivery failures, many complaints from one list, an owner who says they are a minor or not the owner.
