<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** call a customer back about an open or escalated case, give them the outcome, and agree the next step.

**Use it when:** a callback was promised, a Tier 2 decision is ready, or a case is better explained by phone.

**Goal:** the customer hears the outcome clearly and agrees the next step; the ticket is updated.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you dial

- [ ]  Full ticket history read: issue, every promise, every previous contact
- [ ]  The outcome or decision confirmed and ready (refund issued, replacement sent, exception approved or declined)
- [ ]  Callback time agreed with the customer, in their time zone
- [ ]  Calling hours respected: only within the client's allowed hours in the customer's local time
- [ ]  Number on the ticket checked; do-not-call preferences on the account respected
- [ ]  Client's recording disclosure ready, if required

## The flow

### 1. Introduce yourself and the reason

> **You:** Hi, is this (CUSTOMER NAME)?
**Them:** Yes, speaking.
**You:** Hi (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME), this is (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME) support. I'm calling about your (ISSUE), as we arranged. Is now still a good time?
> 

*Why it works: honest, clear and polite. You ask permission before continuing.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If it is not a good time</summary>

> **You:** No problem. When would be better? (Agree a time.) I'll call you then, and I'll also send a short email with the update in the meantime.
>

</details>

### 2. Verify (if account details will be discussed)

> **You:** Before I go into the details, could you confirm (VERIFICATION DETAILS)?
> 

### 3. Outcome first

**Good news:**

> **You:** I have good news: our senior team approved a full refund of (AMOUNT). It went back to your card ending in (LAST FOUR DIGITS) this morning, and banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.
> 

**Not the outcome they hoped for:**

> **You:** I've got an answer from our senior team, and I want to be straightforward with you: they weren't able to approve (REQUEST). What they can offer is (ALTERNATIVE). Would that work for you?
> 

*Why it works: the outcome comes first; no long build-up that raises false hope.*

### 4. Agree the next step

> **You:** So the next step is (WHAT), and you'll (receive / see) it by (DATE). Does that work for you?
> 

### 5. Close

> **You:** I'll email you a summary right after this call. Is there anything else I can help with? Thank you, (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME).
> 

## Voicemail

If the customer does not answer, leave a short message with no private details:

> **You:** Hi, this is (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME) support, calling (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME) about your recent request, as arranged. I've also sent you an email with the update. You can reply there, or call us at (SUPPORT NUMBER). Thank you.
> 

Then send the email update at once.

## Objections

| Customer says | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "How do I know you're really from (COMPANY NAME)?" | "Good question. You can hang up and call our support line at (SUPPORT NUMBER), or check the email I've just sent from our support address." |
| "That's not good enough." (after a "no") | Use STEER. "I understand you hoped for more. What I can do is (ALTERNATIVE). If you'd like, I can ask the senior team to review once more with any new information you have." |
| "Why didn't you call yesterday?" | If a promise was missed: "You're right, we should have, and I'm sorry." Do not invent reasons. |

## After the call

**Call log (within 5 minutes):**

> Call (outbound callback), (DATE, TIME AND ZONE). Reached (yes or voicemail). Verified (yes or no). Outcome shared: (WHAT). Customer response: (ACCEPTED OR DECLINED). Next: (WHAT, WHEN). Summary email sent (TIME).
> 

**Status:** Solved if accepted and complete; Open with an owner if more is needed.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never call outside the client's allowed calling hours in the customer's local time, never call a customer who has asked not to be called, and never leave account, payment or order details in a voicemail.

</div>
