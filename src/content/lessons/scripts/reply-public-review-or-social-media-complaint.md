<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** respond to public complaints and reviews in a way that helps the customer and reassures everyone else reading.

**Use it when:** a customer posts a complaint or review on a review site or social media, and the client has authorized support to respond publicly.

**Goal:** the problem moves to a private channel to be solved, and the public reply shows the business cares.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Client has approved public replies by support, and the approved tone and sign-off
- [ ]  Customer found in the system (order, tickets) if possible, without sharing anything publicly
- [ ]  Any open ticket linked
- [ ]  Wording approved by Tier 2 or the client for negative posts gaining attention (likes, shares, many replies)

## The flow

### 1. Public reply: short, warm, no private details

> **You:** Hi (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME), I'm sorry to read this. This isn't the experience we want for anyone. We'd like to fix it: please send us a private message with your order number, or email (SUPPORT EMAIL), and we'll take care of it today. (YOUR FIRST NAME), (COMPANY NAME) Support
> 

*Why it works: public empathy, private solution. Readers see a business that responds.*

### 2. Private follow up (once they make contact)

Use the right reply for the issue (late order, refund, damaged item), with the STEER Method if they are upset.

### 3. Optional public close (after it is solved, if the client wants it)

> **You:** Thanks for letting us sort this out, (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME). Glad your replacement arrived.
> 

Never ask the customer to change or remove their review.

<details class="toggle"><summary>Positive review</summary>

> **You:** Thank you, (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME)! We're so glad (SPECIFIC THING THEY PRAISED). We'll pass your kind words to the team.
> 

Be specific to their review, never generic.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Review that seems false or not from a customer</summary>

Do not accuse publicly. Reply politely:

> **You:** Hi, we couldn't find an order matching your name. We'd really like to help, so please message us with your order details.
> 

Report it to the client, who decides whether to flag it with the platform.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Post with legal threats, safety claims or media attention</summary>

Do not reply. Escalate as Urgent per the Escalation Ladder. The client decides the response.

</details>

## Objections and tricky replies

| Customer posts | You reply (publicly) |
| --- | --- |
| "Don't DM me, answer here!" | "Understood. We've looked into it and (GENERAL FACT WITHOUT PRIVATE DATA, for example 'a replacement is on its way'). We've also sent you the details privately." |
| Many angry replies from other customers | Escalate to Tier 2. One calm public reply, then private messages; never argue in the thread. |
| Sarcastic or mocking post | Same short, sincere reply. Never match the tone. |

## After you send

**Internal note on the linked ticket:**

> Public post on (PLATFORM TYPE) (DATE). Public reply sent (TIME). Customer contacted privately (yes or no). Issue: (SUMMARY). Next: (WHAT).
> 

**Tags:** public complaint, platform type, root cause. **Priority:** High.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never share order details, names, addresses or account information in a public reply, never argue or correct the customer publicly, and never offer anything in exchange for changing a review.
