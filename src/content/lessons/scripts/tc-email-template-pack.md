<div class="callout callout-intro">

Purpose: 20 ready emails for the moments every TC faces, from opening title to post-closing.

When to use: any time you need to send a standard TC email. Copy the template, fill every placeholder, and read it once aloud before sending.

Goal of each email: one clear request or update, with a clear next step and a date.

Version: v1.0

</div>

## Before you dial or send

- [ ]  Correct file open in the CRM. Check the address twice; the most common TC email error is the wrong property.
- [ ]  Reply on the existing file thread when there is one. Start a new thread only for a new file or a new topic.
- [ ]  Subject line format: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · (TOPIC)
- [ ]  Every placeholder filled. Search the draft for an opening parenthesis before you send.
- [ ]  Attachments named per page 11 and opened once to check they are the right version.
- [ ]  Recipients checked: copy only the people the client wants copied. Never copy the end buyer on seller emails, or the seller on buyer emails.
- [ ]  No Social Security numbers, bank details or wiring instructions in any email you write.

## Email writing rules

1. **One email, one purpose.** If you have three unrelated requests for title, number them in one email. If you have a seller update and a buyer update, write two emails.
2. **Requests first, details after.** Busy people read the first three lines.
3. **Ask for a reply.** End every request with what you need and by when.
4. **Short and warm.** Thank people by name. No slang, no long apologies.
5. **Confirm calls in writing.** After any important call, send a two line summary so there is a record.

## The flow: templates by stage

### Opening

<details class="toggle"><summary>1. Opening email: preferred title company</summary>

**When:** Day 0, within 4 business hours of a fully signed contract.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · New File · (DEAL TYPE) · Close (CLOSING DATE)
Hi (CLOSER NAME),
We have a new file for you. Attached: signed purchase agreement, (OTHER DOCUMENTS).
Please:
1. Open the file and order the title search and all required city, county and state lien searches.
2. Confirm the deposit of (DEPOSIT AMOUNT) when received (due (DEPOSIT DEADLINE)), by email or escrow receipt.
3. Reply today to confirm you have the file.
Seller: (SELLER NAME) · (SELLER PHONE) · (SELLER EMAIL)
Buyer: (BUYER ENTITY NAME) · (BUYER CONTACT NAME) · (BUYER PHONE) · (BUYER EMAIL)
Lender: (LENDER NAME) · (LENDER COMPANY) · (LENDER PHONE) · (LENDER EMAIL)
Agent: (AGENT NAME) · (AGENT PHONE) · (AGENT EMAIL)
Target closing: (CLOSING DATE)
Thank you, and I look forward to working on this one with you.
(YOUR NAME) · Transaction Coordinator, (COMPANY NAME) · (YOUR PHONE)
> 

Delete any contact line that does not apply.

</details>

### Deposit

<details class="toggle"><summary>5. Request deposit receipt from title</summary>

**When:** On the deposit deadline morning, or when the payer says it was sent.

> Subject: RE: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Deposit receipt
Hi (CLOSER NAME),
The (BUYER ENTITY NAME) deposit of (DEPOSIT AMOUNT) was sent on (SENT DATE) (reference (REFERENCE)). Could you confirm receipt and send the escrow receipt for our file?
Thank you,
(YOUR NAME)
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>6. Seller memo: deposit received and your closing company</summary>

**When:** Same day the deposit is receipted, after the seller call.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Deposit received
Hi (SELLER FIRST NAME),
As we discussed on the phone, the closing company has received the deposit, and your file is officially open.
Closing company: (TITLE COMPANY)
Contact: (CLOSER NAME) · (TITLE PHONE) · (TITLE EMAIL)
They may send you a short form asking for your details. Please return it directly to them. Never send your Social Security number or bank details by text or email to anyone except the closing company through their secure process.
I will call you again on (NEXT CALL DAY).
Warm regards,
(YOUR NAME)
>

</details>

### Title progress

<details class="toggle"><summary>7. Seller document request (title needs something)</summary>

Thank you for your time on the phone. To keep us on track for (CLOSING DATE), the closing company needs:

If anything is unclear, call me and I will help.
Thank you,
(YOUR NAME)

</details>

### Changes and endings

<details class="toggle"><summary>12. Extension sent for signature</summary>

**When:** After the client approves a new closing date.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Extension for signature
Hi (PARTY NAME),
As discussed, we need a little more time to complete (REASON). The extension moves the closing date to (NEW CLOSING DATE). All other terms stay the same.
You will receive it from our e-signature tool shortly. Please sign by (SIGN BY DATE), before the current closing date of (CURRENT CLOSING DATE).
Thank you for your patience. Call me with any questions.
(YOUR NAME)
> 

After signing, send it to title, and remember the end buyer's contract needs a matching extension.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>13. Addendum sent for signature</summary>

**When:** After the client approves a change to the contract terms.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Addendum for signature
Hi (PARTY NAME),
As discussed, this addendum records the following change: (CHANGE IN ONE SENTENCE). Everything else in the contract stays the same.
Please sign by (SIGN BY DATE). If you have any questions about it, call me before signing.
Thank you,
(YOUR NAME)
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>14. Cancellation and release sent for signature</summary>

**When:** Only on the client's written instruction.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Release documents
Hi (PARTY NAME),
As discussed with (CLIENT CONTACT NAME), attached are the documents to end the contract for (PROPERTY ADDRESS) and to instruct (TITLE COMPANY) how to handle the deposit.
Please review and sign by (SIGN BY DATE). If you have questions about the documents, please contact (CLIENT CONTACT NAME) or your own advisor.
Thank you,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

Keep this email factual. Never add opinions about who is at fault.

</details>

### Safety and post-closing

<details class="toggle"><summary>19. Wire fraud warning to all parties</summary>

**When:** Send at opening to the seller and buyer, and again immediately if anyone reports a suspicious email.

> Subject: (PROPERTY ADDRESS) · Important: protect your money
Hi (PARTY NAME),
Criminals sometimes send fake emails during real estate closings, pretending to be the closing company and asking people to send money to a new account.
Please remember:
1. (COMPANY NAME) will never email you wiring instructions.
2. Wiring instructions come only from (TITLE COMPANY).
3. Before sending any money, call (TITLE COMPANY) at a phone number you found yourself, for example on their official website, and confirm the instructions.
4. If you receive any message changing wiring instructions, do not send money. Call (TITLE COMPANY) and me right away.
Thank you for helping us keep your closing safe.
(YOUR NAME)
>

</details>

## Objections

When a reply pushes back, use these short answers and then follow the right page.

| Reply | Your answer | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We need more time." | "Thank you. What date can you commit to, and what is the reason?" | Escalate per page 01 |
| "That is not our policy." | "Understood. Could you tell me what your process is so I can follow it?" | Tell the client the same day |
| "I already sent that." | "Thank you. Could you resend it on this thread so we have it with the file?" | Check spam and other threads |
| No reply after 4 business hours | Call, then reply on the same thread: "Following up on my call." | Escalate if a deadline is near |
