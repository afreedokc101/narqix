<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** book a meeting between the client and an outside contact quickly, with no back and forth.

**Use it when:** the client asks you to set up a meeting, or an outside contact asks to meet the client.

**Goal:** a confirmed time on both calendars in one or two emails.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Client's calendar rules checked (hours, buffers, meeting length)
- [ ]  Contact's time zone found (signature, website, or ask)
- [ ]  Two or three options chosen and **held** on the client's calendar
- [ ]  Meeting purpose and length confirmed with the client
- [ ]  You are signing as yourself, as part of the client's team

## The flow

### 1. Subject line

> **You:** Scheduling: (CLIENT NAME) and (CONTACT NAME), (TOPIC)
> 

### 2. Opening: who you are and why you are writing

> **You:** Hi (CONTACT FIRST NAME),
I am (YOUR NAME), and I help (CLIENT NAME) with scheduling. (CLIENT FIRST NAME) asked me to set up a 25-minute call to discuss (TOPIC).
> 

*Why it works: honest about who you are and why you are writing, without long introductions.*

### 3. Specific options in their time zone

> **You:** Would one of these times work for you? All times are in your time zone (CT):
1. Tuesday, 20 October, 10:00 AM CT
2. Wednesday, 21 October, 2:00 PM CT
3. Thursday, 22 October, 11:30 AM CT
> 

*Why it works: three specific choices get a reply far faster than "when are you free?". Writing the day of the week and the month as words prevents date confusion.*

### 4. Make it easy to answer

> **You:** Just reply with 1, 2 or 3, and I will send the invite with a video link. If none work, send me two times that suit you and I will make it happen.
Best regards,
(YOUR NAME)
Office of (CLIENT NAME), (COMPANY NAME)
> 

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they pick a time</summary>

> **You:** Thank you, (CONTACT FIRST NAME). Confirmed for Wednesday, 21 October, 2:00 PM CT. The invite with the video link is on its way.
> 

Convert the hold into the real event with the Five Facts, delete the other holds the same day, and tell the client in the digest.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they suggest a time outside the client&#39;s rules</summary>

> **You:** Thank you. That time is not available on (CLIENT FIRST NAME)'s side. The closest options are Friday 9:00 AM CT or Monday 1:00 PM CT. Would either work?
> 

Never break a protected block without the client's approval. If the contact is a VIP, ask the client first.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they send their own booking link</summary>

Check with the client whether you may book through outside links. If yes, book a slot that fits the client's rules, then confirm by email and add the event to the client's calendar with the Five Facts.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If there is no reply after 2 business days</summary>

> **You:** Hi (CONTACT FIRST NAME), following up on the call with (CLIENT FIRST NAME). The options below are still open until Friday. Reply with a number and I will lock it in.
> 

Keep the holds 24 hours after the nudge, then release them and tell the client.

</details>

## Tricky replies

| Contact says | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Who are you?" | "I am (YOUR NAME), part of (CLIENT NAME)'s team. I manage the schedule so (CLIENT FIRST NAME) can focus on the meeting itself." |
| "Can (CLIENT) just call me now?" | "I will pass that on right away. If now is not possible, here are the earliest times." (Message the client.) |
| "Can we make it 60 minutes?" | "Let me check with (CLIENT FIRST NAME) and come back to you by (TIME AND ZONE)." |

## After you send

- Holds placed and titled "HOLD: (TOPIC) with (CONTACT NAME)".
- Email moved to 03 Waiting On with a follow up date.
- Once confirmed: invite sent, holds cleared, digest updated.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never pretend to be the client, and never invent a reason the client is unavailable. "That time is not available" is honest and enough.

</div>
