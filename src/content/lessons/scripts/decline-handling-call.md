<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** tell a merchant, kindly and honestly, that their file cannot move forward right now, and give them real, honest alternatives and a path back.

**When to use:** when a merchant does not meet any product's screening minimums during qualification, or when the desk lead confirms a file is declined or will not be submitted.

**Goal of the call:** one outcome: the merchant understands where they stand, feels respected, and agrees (or declines) a clear next step: a re-check date, another product path, or credit repair.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you dial

- [ ]  The decline reason confirmed by the desk lead (or the specific screening minimum not met, if during qualification)
- [ ]  The reason code and the wording you are allowed to share (your lead tells you what may be shared)
- [ ]  Honest alternatives identified with your lead: another product, a re-check date, credit repair, or none
- [ ]  Consent and calling window confirmed

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never blame the merchant, never say "you'll definitely get approved next time", never suggest changing answers or documents to qualify ("if you just showed more deposits..."), never refer them to a company you know nothing about, and never ask for any fee for "help". If you are not allowed to share a funder's reason, do not invent one.

</div>

## The flow

### 1. Opening

> **You:** Hi (MERCHANT NAME), this is (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). This call may be recorded for quality. I'm calling with an update on your (PRODUCT) file. Is now a good time?
> 

### 2. Deliver the news clearly

Say it early, in plain words. Do not make them wait through small talk.

> **You:** I'm sorry to say that we're not able to move your file forward right now.
**You:** The main reason is (APPROVED REASON IN PLAIN WORDS).
> 

Examples of plain, approved reasons:

- "The programs we work with need at least (MINIMUM) months in business, and you're at (NUMBER)."
- "Monthly deposits need to be a bit higher than they are right now for these programs."
- "With the number of advances currently on the account, the funders we work with aren't able to add another one."
- "There's an open item, (BANKRUPTCY, TAX LIEN, JUDGMENT), that needs to be resolved first."

*Why it works: clear bad news, delivered early and kindly, is more respectful than vague hope. Merchants can plan around a clear answer.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Why? Other companies said I was approved&quot;</summary>

> **You:** I understand that's frustrating. I can only tell you what the funders we work with have said. Nobody is approved until a funder reviews the file and makes an offer in writing. If someone has given you a real written offer, read it carefully and make the decision that's right for you.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they get upset</summary>

> **You:** I hear you, and I'm sorry. You put time into this, and it's not the answer you wanted. Let me tell you what I think could help from here.
> 

Stay calm and kind. If they become abusive, end the call politely and note it. If they mention a lawyer, complaint or regulator, listen, note their words and escalate.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the funder&#39;s reason may not be shared</summary>

> **You:** The funder didn't share a detailed reason with us that I can pass on. What I can tell you is what usually helps files like yours, and when it makes sense for us to look again.
>

</details>

### 3. Offer honest alternatives

Choose only the options that genuinely fit. If none fit, say so.

<details class="toggle"><summary>Alternative 1: A re-check date</summary>

> **You:** Time and history help a lot. Once you reach (MILESTONE, FOR EXAMPLE 6 MONTHS IN BUSINESS), things may look different. Would it be OK if we checked back in around (MONTH)?
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Alternative 2: A different product</summary>

> **You:** Based on what you told me, (OTHER PRODUCT) may be a better fit than (ORIGINAL PRODUCT). It works differently: (ONE-LINE PLAIN DESCRIPTION). Would you like me to have the team take a look?
> 

Examples: an owner with strong personal credit but a new business might fit 0% Business Credit Cards (with full card disclosures). A business waiting on invoices from other businesses might fit Invoice Factoring. A merchant buying one machine might fit Equipment Lending.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Alternative 3: Credit repair</summary>

> **You:** Credit seems to be holding things back. We offer a credit repair service that reviews your credit reports and disputes items that are wrong, outdated or can't be verified. I want to be clear: results vary, nobody can promise a higher score, and accurate information can't be removed. Would you like someone from that team to explain how it works?
> 

The company handles contracts, disclosures and any fees. You never quote a fee or a result.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Alternative 4: Talk to their bank, or wait</summary>

> **You:** Honestly, sometimes the best option is no new funding right now. If you have a relationship with your bank, it's worth asking them too. Banks are often the lowest-cost option when they can say yes.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Alternative 5: Heavy existing advances</summary>

> **You:** You mentioned your current payments are heavy. Adding more funding usually makes that harder, not easier. Would you like someone from our team to talk with you about whether our MCA Bailout program could help? I can't promise it will lower your payments, and any option would show you the total cost side by side before you decide.
> 

If they mention default or stopping payments, escalate the same day. Never advise them to stop paying a funder.

</details>

*Why it works: an honest alternative turns a "no" into trust. A merchant who was treated well after a decline often comes back, or sends a friend.*

### 4. Agree the next step

> **You:** So, to confirm: (NEXT STEP). I'll note that, and (WHO) will reach out (WHEN). Is the best way to reach you still (CHANNEL)?
> 

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Don&#39;t contact me again&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Understood. I'll remove you from our list. Thank you for your time, and I wish you and the business all the best.
> 

Process the opt-out in every system immediately.

</details>

### 5. Close

> **You:** Thank you for your patience, (MERCHANT NAME). I'm sorry it wasn't better news today. If anything changes before then, you can always reach us at (COMPANY PHONE).
> 

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: never fill silence after bad news with promises. Let them react. A short "I understand" is better than a long explanation. Merchants remember how you made them feel more than the exact reason.

</div>
