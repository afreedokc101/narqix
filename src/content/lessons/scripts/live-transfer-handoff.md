<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** move a fully qualified homeowner to a US energy consultant on the same call, with a warm introduction.

**When to use:** after stages 1 to 4 of the Fresh Web Lead Setter Call or the Inbound Solar Call, when the campaign runs live transfers and a consultant is available.

**Goal of the call:** the consultant accepts the homeowner and starts the conversation, or a firm appointment is booked if no consultant is free.

**Version:** v1.0

Track: Solar  ·  Role: Live Transfer Agent  ·  Time to complete: 15 min to learn

</div>

## Before you transfer

- [ ]  Every qualification gate answered and written in the CRM
- [ ]  All decision makers on the line or nearby, or the homeowner confirmed they decide alone
- [ ]  Consultant availability checked (queue, line or team channel, as your campaign works)
- [ ]  Campaign hold-time limit known
- [ ]  Two fallback appointment slots ready on the calendar

<div class="callout callout-tip">

**The transfer summary** is the heart of this script. Practice it until you can say it calmly in about 20 seconds: name, city, ownership, bill, utility, roof, shade, decision makers, motivation.

</div>

## The flow

### 1. Offer the transfer

> **You:** "Thank you, (HOMEOWNER NAME). From everything you told me, it makes sense for a consultant to look at your options. (CONSULTANT NAME) is available right now. Would you like me to bring them on the line so you can talk it through together?"
> 

*Why it works: it is a clear, honest choice. The homeowner agrees to the next step instead of being pushed into it.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;Yes, sure.&quot;</summary>

Go to stage 2.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;Not right now.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "No problem. I can book a time for you both instead. (CONSULTANT NAME) has (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2). Which works better?"
> 

Book it as a normal set.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;My husband needs to be part of it, and he is not home.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "Then let us find a time when you are both there. That way he hears everything first hand. (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2)?"
> 

Never transfer with a decision maker missing.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;Will they tell me the price and how much I will save?&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "They will go through the real numbers for your home and the options available. I cannot promise any particular result, because it depends on your roof and your usage, but they will be straight with you."
>

</details>

### 2. Set expectations for the hold

> **You:** "Great. I will place you on a short hold while I connect (CONSULTANT NAME). I will stay on the line and introduce you, so you will not need to repeat anything. It should only take a moment."
> 

### 3. Brief the consultant (private, if your phone system allows)

> **You:** "Hi (CONSULTANT NAME), it is (YOUR NAME) from the (COMPANY NAME) scheduling team. I have (HOMEOWNER NAME) from (CITY), (STATE) on hold. Homeowner, (YEARS) years in the home, bill about (AMOUNT) a month with (UTILITY NAME), roof about (YEARS) years, (SHADE LEVEL) shade. (SPOUSE NAME) is with them. Main reason: (THEIR WORDS). Ready for me to bring them in?"
> 

*Why it works: the consultant has everything they need before saying hello.*

### 4. Check in during the hold

If the consultant does not pick up quickly, return to the homeowner about every 30 seconds:

> **You:** "Thank you for waiting, (HOMEOWNER NAME). I am still connecting you. Just a few more moments."
> 

### 5. The three-way introduction

> **You:** "(HOMEOWNER NAME), I have (CONSULTANT NAME) on the line with us. (CONSULTANT NAME), (HOMEOWNER NAME) and (SPOUSE NAME) own their home in (CITY). Their bill runs about (AMOUNT) a month, the roof is about (YEARS) years old, and what matters most to them is (THEIR WORDS). (HOMEOWNER NAME), (CONSULTANT NAME) is the consultant I mentioned. I will let you two talk."
> 

*Why it works: the homeowner hears that you listened, and the consultant starts from the homeowner's real reason.*

### 6. Hand over and leave

Wait until the consultant speaks and the homeowner replies. Then:

> **You:** "Thank you both. Have a great conversation."
> 

Leave the call.

### 7. Fallback: no consultant available

If the hold limit is reached:

> **You:** "(HOMEOWNER NAME), thank you for your patience. Our consultants are all with other homeowners right now, and I do not want to keep you waiting. Let me book you a firm time instead. (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2)?"
> 

Book, lock and confirm as in the Fresh Web Lead Setter Call, stage 6.

### 8. If the call drops

Call back once, immediately:

> **You:** "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), it is (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME) again. I think we got cut off. Shall I try connecting you again, or would you prefer to book a time?"
> 

No answer: leave a short voicemail (V1 adapted) and send a text with two appointment options.

## After the call

1. Code LT if the consultant accepted, SET if you booked a fallback, CB or NUR-(REASON) otherwise.
2. Note:

> **LT / (DATE) (TIME) (TIME ZONE) / to (CONSULTANT NAME)**
Owners: (NAMES), on call: (WHO). Avg bill about (AMOUNT), utility (NAME), roof (DETAIL), shade (LEVEL).
Motivation: (THEIR WORDS). Hold time: (SECONDS).
Next: check outcome by end of shift. (YOUR NAME)
> 
1. Log the transfer in your tracker: attempted, accepted, fallback booked, or dropped.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Slow down in the introduction. Under pressure, agents rush the summary and the consultant misses half of it. Breathe, say the name, and give one fact per sentence.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:**

Never transfer without the homeowner's agreement.

Never transfer a partly qualified homeowner to "save time".

Never leave the line before the consultant has clearly taken over.

Never say "You will definitely save" or "They will get you approved".

</div>

## Check yourself

1. List the facts in the private consultant brief.
2. What do you say every 30 seconds during a hold?
3. The hold limit is reached. Write your fallback line.
4. When exactly do you leave the three-way call?
5. Which code do you use if you booked a fallback appointment?
