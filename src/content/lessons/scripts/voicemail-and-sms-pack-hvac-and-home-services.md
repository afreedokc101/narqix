<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** Ready-to-use voicemails, prerecorded voicemail drops and texts for every common home services situation, including first-touch texts to cold lists. All short, clear and compliant.

**When to use:** Whenever a call goes to voicemail or a text touch is due. Always use your client's approved version first if it has one.

**Goal of the call:** The customer knows who called, why, and exactly how to respond.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you dial

- [ ]  Customer's first name, the reason for contact, and the right template below
- [ ]  Opt-out status checked
- [ ]  Allowed hours for the customer's time zone (texts follow calling hours)
- [ ]  Sending from the client's registered texting platform only
- [ ]  Links (review, estimate, financing) tested before sending

## Rules for every voicemail

1. Your name, the company name, the reason, and a callback number. Say the number twice, slowly.
2. Under 30 seconds.
3. No sensitive details: no prices, no health information, no account details.
4. Leave a live, personal voicemail, or use a prerecorded voicemail drop from the set below, recorded in the client's approved wording.

## Rules for every text

1. Company name in the message.
2. About 160 to 220 characters, plain words, one clear question or action.
3. Opt-out line ("Reply STOP to opt out") on the first message of any thread and on all marketing texts.
4. No ALL CAPS, no fake urgency, no misleading links.
5. Never ask for card numbers, bank details or Social Security numbers by text.

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never text from a personal phone. Never send marketing texts to anyone who has opted out, or to a cold number that is not on the client's approved, DNC-scrubbed cold list. Never send texts outside allowed hours, even if they are "just reminders".

</div>

## Prerecorded voicemail drops

Drops are recorded once in the client's approved wording and sent through the dialer when a call reaches voicemail. Use them for cold lists, storm area lists, seasonal campaigns and old estimates.

- [ ]  Recording made from the client's approved wording, calm and slow, under 30 seconds
- [ ]  Company name said, callback number said twice
- [ ]  Opt-out line at the end
- [ ]  Only to the client's approved, DNC-scrubbed list, inside allowed hours
- [ ]  One drop per number per day at most. Log it in the CRM, for example "DROP D2"

## Objections

Texts often get one-line replies. Common ones:

| They reply | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Who is this?" | "Hi, it's (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME). (REASON FOR CONTACT). Is now a good time?" |
| "How much?" | "Good question. The visit is (DIAGNOSTIC FEE), and the technician gives you the exact repair price before doing any work. Want me to book a time?" |
| "Wrong number" | "Sorry about that! We'll update our records." Then opt out and note it |
| "Not interested" | "No problem, thanks for letting us know." Then stop and note it; opt out if they ask not to be contacted |

## Check yourself

1. Name four things every voicemail must include.
2. Which messages must carry the opt-out line?
3. Why say the callback number twice?
4. A customer replies "wrong number". What are your two actions?

<details class="toggle toggle-answers"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Your name, the company name, the reason, and a callback number.
2. The first message of any thread and all marketing texts (and follow your client's rule for others).
3. So the customer can write it down easily, especially if they are listening in a noisy place.
4. Apologise briefly, and opt the number out and note it in the record.

</details>
