<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** confirm external meetings the day before, move meetings politely, and recover from a no-show.

**Use it when:** about 24 hours before every external meeting, when either side needs to move, or when someone misses a meeting.

**Goal:** every meeting happens as planned, or is moved with a new confirmed time.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Meeting details checked in the calendar (time, zone, link or address)
- [ ]  Contact's time zone known
- [ ]  For a reschedule: two or three new options held on the client's calendar
- [ ]  For a VIP: client has approved the change

## The flow

### 1. Day-before confirmation

> **You:** Subject: Confirming tomorrow: (CLIENT NAME) and (CONTACT NAME), 2:00 PM CT
Hi (CONTACT FIRST NAME),
A quick confirmation for tomorrow, Wednesday, 2:00 PM CT, with (CLIENT FIRST NAME). The video link is in the invite and here: (MEETING LINK).
If anything has changed, just reply and I will find a new time.
Best regards,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

*Why it works: short, gives the link again, and makes changing easy so people reschedule instead of not showing up.*

### 2. Reschedule request from the client's side

> **You:** Subject: New time needed: (TOPIC) call
Hi (CONTACT FIRST NAME),
I am sorry, but (CLIENT FIRST NAME) needs to move Wednesday's call. Would one of these work instead? All times CT:
1. Thursday, 10:00 AM
2. Thursday, 3:30 PM
3. Friday, 9:00 AM
Reply with a number and I will update the invite right away.
> 

*Why it works: one short apology, no made-up excuse, and ready options.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the contact asks why it moved</summary>

> **You:** Something came up on (CLIENT FIRST NAME)'s side that could not be moved. They are keen to speak with you this week.
> 

Keep it honest and simple. Never invent a reason.

</details>

### 3. Reschedule request from the contact

> **You:** No problem at all, (CONTACT FIRST NAME). Here are the next openings, CT:
1. Friday, 11:00 AM
2. Monday, 2:00 PM
Reply with a number and I will send the updated invite.
> 

### 4. No-show: 10 minutes after start

> **You:** Subject: We missed you today
Hi (CONTACT FIRST NAME),
(CLIENT FIRST NAME) was on the call at 2:00 PM CT but we did not see you. I hope everything is fine. Would you like to pick a new time? Options:
1. Thursday, 10:00 AM CT
2. Friday, 1:00 PM CT
> 

*Why it works: warm, no blame, and gives a path back.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If it is the second no-show</summary>

Do not offer new times automatically. Tell the client: "(CONTACT NAME) missed the second call. Would you like me to offer one more time, or leave it with them to reach out?"

</details>

## Tricky replies

| Contact says | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Can we do it by phone instead?" | "Of course. What is the best number to reach you on?" Update the invite with the number and tell the client. |
| "Running 10 minutes late." | Tell the client immediately in their urgent channel, then reply: "Thanks for letting us know. (CLIENT FIRST NAME) will wait until 2:10 PM CT." (Check first how long the client will wait.) |
| "I need to cancel completely." | "Understood, thank you for letting us know. If you would like to reconnect later, just reply to this email." |

## After you send

- Calendar event updated with "Confirmed (DATE)" in the description, or the new time.
- Old holds released; new holds placed for proposed options.
- No-shows logged in the tracker and mentioned in the evening brief.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: the day-before confirmation is not a formality. It is the single best way to cut no-shows, because people who would have forgotten will either show up or reschedule. Send it every time.

</div>
