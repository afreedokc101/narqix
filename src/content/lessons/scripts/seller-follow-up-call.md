<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** Re-engage a seller who said "not now," "let me think," or did not decide after an offer, at 1 week, 30 days and 90 days.

**When to use:** Scheduled follow-ups on FU-WARM, OFFER, NEG and NI leads, inside the calling window and the client's follow-up cadence.

**Goal of the call:** One outcome. Learn what has changed, and either book the next real step (discovery, offer call or new offer) or set the next follow-up date with the seller's agreement.

**Version:** v1.0. Includes the 1 week, 30 day and 90 day versions.

Track: Real Estate  ·  Role: Lead Manager, Acquisitions  ·  Time to complete: 25 min

</div>

## Why follow-up wins deals

Most sellers do not sell on the first call. Life changes: a tenant leaves, a repair bill arrives, a family decision is made. The company that stays in touch politely, remembers the details, and calls at the right moment usually gets the deal. The one that pushes gets blocked.

| Version | Who it is for | Main goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 week | Seller received an offer or had discovery, and said "let me think" | Answer questions, learn the real blocker, move to a decision |
| 30 day | Seller said "not now" with some motivation | Check what changed, keep the relationship warm |
| 90 day | Seller was not interested, or went quiet | Light check-in, see if timing changed, ask permission to stay in touch |

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Every follow-up must have a reason that is about the seller, not about you. "You mentioned your tenant's lease ends in March" beats "just checking in" every time. Read the notes, find the hook, and open with it.

</div>

## Follow-up rules

- Only call leads that are not on any DNC list. If a seller ever asked not to be contacted, they are never on a follow-up list.
- Respect the calling window in the seller's local time and the 3 attempts per 24 hours cap, across calls and texts.
- Text only sellers who gave texting consent, through the registered texting platform.
- Every follow-up, wine and dine them again with the notes from your first call. Find the common ground you had and open with it.
- If a seller says "please stop following up," it is a DNC request. Honor it immediately.

## Before you dial

- [ ]  Full CRM history read: every note, every offer, the seller's words on motivation, and the reason they gave for waiting.
- [ ]  Your hook ready: one specific thing from the notes.
- [ ]  If an offer was made: the offer, the counter, and whether your manager approved anything new.
- [ ]  Calling window, attempt cap and DNC status checked.

## Version 1: The 1 week follow-up

### 1. Open with the hook

> **You:** Hi (SELLER FIRST NAME), it's (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). This call is recorded for quality. Do you have a couple of minutes?
> 

> **You:** Last week we talked about (STREET NAME), and you wanted some time to (THEIR REASON, FOR EXAMPLE TALK WITH YOUR SON). How did that go?
> 

*Why it works: You remember exactly what they said. It shows you listened and makes the call about their decision, not your deal.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;We&#39;re still thinking&quot;</summary>

> **You:** That's fine. Can I ask what part you're still thinking about? The price, the timing, or something else?
> 

> **You:** Is there anything I can answer or send that would help?
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;We decided not to sell&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Thank you for letting me know. Can I ask what made you decide?
> 

> **You:** Would it be okay if I checked in with you in a few months, in case anything changes?
> 

If yes, code **NI** with the follow-up date. If no, code **NI** with no follow-up. If they ask you to stop, code **DNC**.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;We sold to someone else&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Congratulations. I'm glad it worked out for you. Do you own any other properties you might sell one day?
> 

Code **SOLD**.

</details>

### 2. Find the real blocker

> **You:** If you could change one thing about our offer, what would it be?
> 

Common answers and next steps:

| Blocker | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| "The price" | Ask what number would work and why. Take it to your manager if it is within reason |
| "The timing" | Ask what date works. Closing dates are often flexible |
| "My family isn't sure" | Offer a call with the whole family |
| "I don't trust it" | Offer references, the agreement to read, or attorney review |
| "I want to try listing first" | Respect it. Ask when the listing would end. Set follow-up |

*Why it works: "One thing" is easier to answer than "what's wrong?" It gives you the most important issue.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they name a price</summary>

> **You:** Is that the best you can do, so I can let our underwriter, (APPROVER FIRST NAME), know? And if they can come up to that number, are you ready to move forward today?
> 

> **You:** Let me put you on a quick hold and see what our underwriter can do.
> 

Hold, get the answer from the approver, and come back with a small step, the same way as The Offer Call, stage 9. If your manager is not reachable, book a call back by (TIME).

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Another company offered more&quot;</summary>

> **You:** That's good to know. Did they put it in writing? And what would you take home after their fees and costs, and when would they close?
> 

> **You:** If their offer is really better, you should take it. If anything changes, call me.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Nothing, we&#39;re just not ready&quot;</summary>

> **You:** That's okay. When do you think you might be ready? I'll check in then.
> 

Set the follow-up date with their agreement. Move to the 30 day cadence.

</details>

### 3. Book the next step

> **You:** It sounds like the next step is (NEXT STEP, FOR EXAMPLE A CALL WITH YOUR FAMILY). Does (DAY OPTION 1) or (DAY OPTION 2) work?
> 

## Version 2: The 30 day follow-up

### 1. Open with the hook

> **You:** Hi (SELLER FIRST NAME), it's (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). This call is recorded for quality. We spoke about a month ago about (STREET NAME). Is now an okay time for a quick minute?
> 

> **You:** You mentioned (SPECIFIC DETAIL, FOR EXAMPLE THE TENANT WAS BEHIND ON RENT). How's that going?
> 

> **You:** We're still making offers in your area this week, and I wanted to see if you're still open to selling, or if anything's changed.
> 

*Why it works: Thirty days is enough time for life to change. A specific question gets a real update.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If things got worse</summary>

> **You:** I'm sorry to hear that. Has it changed how you think about selling?
> 

If yes, move to discovery (Seller Discovery and Qualification script) or book an offer call.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If things got better</summary>

> **You:** That's really good to hear. Sounds like holding on is working for now.
> 

> **You:** Would it be okay to check in again in a couple of months, just in case?
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Who is this again?&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Sorry, I'm (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We buy houses in (CITY). We talked last month about your property on (STREET NAME), and you said it might make sense to talk again around now.
>

</details>

### 2. Check what changed

> **You:** Has anything changed with the house, or with your plans?
> 

> **You:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how likely are you to sell in the next six months?
> 

> **You:** What would make it a 10?
> 

*Why it works: The 1 to 10 scale turns a vague "maybe" into something you can track. "What would make it a 10" reveals what needs to happen.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say 7 or higher</summary>

> **You:** That sounds like it's getting closer. Would it help to get an updated offer, so you know where you stand?
> 

Book the offer call or discovery.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say 4 to 6</summary>

> **You:** Got it. What's holding it at a (NUMBER)?
> 

Note the answer and set the next follow-up date with them.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say 3 or lower</summary>

> **You:** Thanks for being honest. I'll check in with you down the road. Is (MONTH) okay?
>

</details>

### 3. Close

> **You:** Thanks, (SELLER FIRST NAME). I'll call you around (DATE). If anything changes before then, you can reach us at (OFFICE NUMBER).
> 

## Version 3: The 90 day follow-up

### 1. Open light

> **You:** Hi (SELLER FIRST NAME), it's (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). This call is recorded for quality. We spoke a few months ago about your property on (STREET NAME). I'm just checking in. Is now a bad time?
> 

> **You:** Last time, selling wasn't right for you. Has anything changed since then?
> 

*Why it works: A light, honest check-in with no pitch. The seller can say "no" easily, which makes them more likely to say "actually..."*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Actually, yes&quot;</summary>

> **You:** I'm glad I called then. What's changed?
> 

Move into discovery. Rerun HOMEKEY, because condition, debt and plans may all be different now.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;No, nothing&#39;s changed&quot;</summary>

> **You:** That's fine. Would it be okay if I checked in once more in a few months? Or would you rather I didn't?
> 

Respect the answer. If they say not to call, code **DNC**.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Stop calling me&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Understood. I'm removing your number now. You won't hear from us again. Sorry for the trouble.
> 

Code **DNC** immediately.

</details>

### 2. Offer a reason to stay in touch

> **You:** If it's helpful, I'm happy to update our offer any time, so you always know your options. No obligation.
> 

> **You:** And if you know anyone else thinking about selling a house, we'd be happy to talk with them.
> 

### 3. Close

> **You:** Thanks for your time, (SELLER FIRST NAME). Take care.
> 

## Re-engaging a seller who said "not now" after an offer

Use this inside any version when an offer was made before.

> **You:** When we last talked, our offer was (LAST OFFER), and you felt (THEIR REACTION). I wanted to see if anything has changed on your side.
> 

If your manager approved a new number for a real reason (market change, new information):

> **You:** We took another look based on (REAL REASON). We can now offer (NEW OFFER). Would you like me to walk you through it?
> 

If nothing changed on your side:

> **You:** Our numbers are about the same. But if your timing or needs have changed, let me take another run at it with the team. Where do you need to be to move forward today?
> 

*Why it works: Reopening the conversation around their number puts the seller back in the approval process, with a fresh hold and a fresh chance to say yes.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Is the offer still good?&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Let me confirm with (MANAGER NAME). Values and repair costs change, so we'd want to recheck the numbers. Can I call you back by (TIME)?
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;Can you do better now?&quot;</summary>

> **You:** I'll fight for you. What number would make it work for you today? And if they can get there, are you ready to sign today?
> 

Then hold and get the answer from your manager.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say &quot;I&#39;ve listed it with an agent&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Good luck with it. I hope it sells well. When does the listing end? If it doesn't sell, we'd still be glad to talk.
> 

Code **LISTED** with the end date. Follow your client's policy.

</details>

## Voicemail and text

If you reach voicemail, leave the matching follow-up voicemail from the Voicemail and Text Pack, only once per attempt cycle. If the seller gave texting consent, send the matching follow-up text.

## Objections

For full handling, see the Objection Handbook in the Script Library.

| If you hear | Short answer |
| --- | --- |
| "You keep calling." | "I'm sorry. Would you like me to stop, or call less often?" (If stop, code DNC.) |
| "Your offer was too low." | "I understand. What would make it work for you?" |
| "I'm still thinking." | "That's okay. What would help you decide?" |
| "Not now." | "No problem. When would be a better time to check in?" |

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this**

Never call a seller who asked not to be contacted, even "just once more."

Never call without reading the notes. "Remind me what's going on with the house?" tells the seller nobody listened.

</div>
