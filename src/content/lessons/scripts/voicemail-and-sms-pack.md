<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** approved wording for every voicemail, prerecorded voicemail drop and text in the solar lead and appointment journey, including first-touch texts to cold lists.

**When to use:** whenever a call goes to voicemail, and at every text touch in the cadence and confirmation sequence.

**Goal of each message:** one clear next step: call back, reply, or keep the appointment.

**Version:** v1.0

Track: Solar  ·  Role: SMS Agent, Appointment Setter, Confirmer, Lead Manager  ·  Time to complete: 20 min to learn

</div>

## Before you send anything

- [ ]  Consent on file for this number, or the number is on the client's approved cold list. No DNC flag either way
- [ ]  Homeowner's local time inside 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday
- [ ]  Sending from the client's registered business number through the texting platform
- [ ]  Message uses the client's company name
- [ ]  First text to this number includes "Reply STOP to opt out"
- [ ]  No links, pictures or attachments unless the client has approved them

## Voicemail rules

1. **Live voicemail or prerecorded drop.** Speak the message yourself, or use a prerecorded voicemail drop from the set below, recorded in the client's approved wording. No AI voices unless the client's legal team has approved them in writing.
2. **Under 25 seconds.** Name, company, reason, one call to action, number twice.
3. **Say the number slowly,** in groups: "five five five, two one two, four four eight nine".
4. **One voicemail per day per lead** at most, inside the attempt cap.
5. **No sales claims.** Never mention prices, savings or incentives.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Smile and slow down for voicemails. A homeowner decides whether to call back from your tone in the first three seconds. Practice each voicemail out loud until it sounds like you, not like a script.

</div>

## Prerecorded voicemail drops

Drops are recorded once and sent through the dialer when a call reaches voicemail. They save time on cold and aged lists and keep every message the same.

**Drop rules**

- [ ]  Use only recordings made from the client's approved wording below
- [ ]  Record in a quiet room, at a calm, slow pace, under 25 seconds
- [ ]  Say the company name, and say the callback number twice
- [ ]  End with the simple opt-out line, so anyone who wants to stop can ask
- [ ]  One drop per lead per day at most, inside the calling window and the attempt cap
- [ ]  Log every drop in the CRM with its code, for example "DROP D1"

<details class="toggle"><summary>D1: Cold list, first touch</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We work with homeowners all over (MARKET) who want to see if solar makes sense for their home. If you would like a quick, no-obligation look at your options, call us back at (CALLBACK NUMBER). Again, that is (CALLBACK NUMBER). If you would rather not hear from us, call that number and we will take you off our list. Thank you."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>D2: Fresh web lead, after a missed live call</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME), following up on your request about solar for your home. We would love to set up a short, no-obligation consultation at a time that suits you. Please call or text us at (CALLBACK NUMBER). That is (CALLBACK NUMBER). To stop hearing from us, just let us know at that number. Thank you."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>D3: Aged lead check-in</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). A while back you asked about solar for your home, and we are checking in to see if it is still on your mind. If it is, call us at (CALLBACK NUMBER). Again, (CALLBACK NUMBER). If not, no problem. Just tell us and we will take you off our list. Thanks."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>D4: Appointment reminder</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME), calling about your upcoming solar consultation. We want to make sure the time still works for you. Please call or text us at (CALLBACK NUMBER). That is (CALLBACK NUMBER). Thank you, and we look forward to seeing you."
>

</details>

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** A drop works best as the first touch of the day on a cold or aged list, followed by a cold text a few minutes later. The homeowner hears your company name, then sees it in writing, and the callback rate goes up.

</div>

## Texting rules

1. **Short.** Aim for under 160 characters. Never more than two text segments.
2. **Company name in every message.**
3. **First message to a number:** add "Reply STOP to opt out."
4. **One question per text,** easy to answer in a word.
5. **No capital-letter shouting, no slang, no pressure words** ("act now", "last chance", "limited").
6. **Never send two unanswered texts in a row on the same day.**
7. **Read every reply before sending the next template.** A reply changes the conversation.

## Texts

### Cold lists (first touch)

Use these for the first text to a number on the client's approved cold list. Send one cold text, then wait for a reply or for the next touch in the cadence.

<details class="toggle"><summary>C1: Cold list, no name on file</summary>

> "Hi, this is (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We help homeowners in (MARKET) see if solar fits their home. Would you like a quick, no-obligation consultation? Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C2: Cold list, name and address on file</summary>

> "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME) here. Are you the owner of the home at (PROPERTY ADDRESS)? We are helping homeowners in (MARKET) look at solar. Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C3: Cold list, after a voicemail drop</summary>

> "Hi, (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We just left you a short voicemail about solar for homeowners in (MARKET). Is it okay to send you a few details here? Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C4: Cold list, second touch (a later day, no reply yet)</summary>

> "Hi, (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME) again. Is solar something you would like to look at for your home this year? A simple yes or no is fine."
>

</details>

### No-shows

<details class="toggle"><summary>S7: No-show, same day</summary>

> "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME). We missed each other today, hope all is well. Would (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2) work better?"
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>S8: No-show, final (day 3)</summary>

> "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME). Happy to find a new time if you are still interested: (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2). If not, no problem, just reply NO and I will close your file."
>

</details>

### Nurture

<details class="toggle"><summary>S10: Nurture check-in</summary>

> "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME). Last time you mentioned (THEIR REASON). Is now a better time to look at solar for your home?"
> 

Examples for (THEIR REASON): "the roof work in spring", "the new electric car", "waiting until your husband was back".

</details>

### Compliance replies

<details class="toggle"><summary>S12: Reply to STOP or any opt-out wording</summary>

> "You are unsubscribed from (COMPANY NAME) messages. You will not receive further texts."
> 

Many texting platforms send this automatically. If yours does, do not send a second message. Code DNC immediately either way.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>S13: Reply to HELP</summary>

> "(COMPANY NAME): solar consultation scheduling. Questions? Call (CALLBACK NUMBER). Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

## Handling replies

| They reply | You do |
| --- | --- |
| "Yes" to a confirmation | Code CONF, thank them briefly |
| A question about price, savings or incentives | "Great question. (CONSULTANT NAME) will go through the real numbers for your home at the consultation." |
| "Who is this?" | "This is (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME), following up on the solar request from your home." |
| "Wrong number" | "Sorry about that, I will update our records." Code WN |
| STOP, "remove me", "do not text", or anything similar | Code DNC immediately. Do not send any sales reply |
| Angry or legal language | Do not reply. Escalate to the team lead |
| "Call me" | Call within minutes if inside the window. If outside, reply with a time inside the window |

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:**

Never text outside 9 AM to 8 PM in the homeowner's time, or on Sundays.

Never text from a personal phone or an unregistered number.

Never send prices, savings, incentive claims, or the words "free", "approved" or "guaranteed".

Never reply with a sales message after any opt-out wording, even if it is not exactly "STOP".

</div>

## After sending

Log every voicemail and text in the CRM as a touch with the template code, for example "VM V1" or "SMS S3". Log every reply word for word.
