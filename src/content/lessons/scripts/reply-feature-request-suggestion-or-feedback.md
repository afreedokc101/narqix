<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** thank customers for ideas and feedback, set honest expectations, and make sure the idea reaches the client.

**Use it when:** a customer suggests a feature, product, improvement, or shares general feedback (good or bad).

**Goal:** the customer feels heard, and the feedback is logged where the client will see it.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Check if the feature already exists (many "requests" are how-to questions)
- [ ]  Check if it is already on a known list or roadmap the client has shared for support use
- [ ]  Note the exact request in the customer's words for the feedback log

## The flow

### 1. Thank them specifically

> **You:** Hi (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME),
Thanks for taking the time to suggest (FEATURE IN THEIR WORDS). It's clear you've thought about how it would help with (THEIR USE CASE).
> 

*Why it works: repeating their idea shows you read and understood it.*

### 2. Honest expectation

> **You:** I've added it to our product feedback list, where the team reviews ideas every (FREQUENCY, if known). I can't promise if or when it'll be built, but every request is read, and requests from customers using the product every day carry real weight.
> 

*Why it works: honest, no false promise, still meaningful.*

### 3. Something useful now (if possible)

> **You:** In the meantime, (WORKAROUND OR CLOSEST FEATURE) may help: (SHORT STEPS OR LINK).
Best,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the feature already exists</summary>

> **You:** Good news: you can already do this. Here's how: (STEPS). It's a little hidden, so thanks for asking. I've also passed on that it's hard to find.
> 

Use the Product or How-To reply format.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If it is positive feedback or a compliment</summary>

> **You:** Thank you, (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME). That's lovely to hear, and I've shared it with the team. (If they mention a person:) I've made sure (NAME) sees it too.
> 

Log it. Clients love seeing compliments in the weekly report.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If it is negative feedback with no request</summary>

> **You:** Thanks for being honest about (ISSUE). I've passed your comments to the team so they can look at it. If there's anything I can fix for you today, just reply here.
> 

If there is a fixable problem hidden in the feedback, treat it as a support ticket.

</details>

## Objections and tricky replies

| Customer says | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "When will it be ready?" | "I don't have a date, and I don't want to guess. If it's released, it'll be announced in (CHANNEL)." |
| "I'll cancel if you don't add it." | "I understand it matters to you, and I've noted that clearly for the team. If you decide to cancel, I can help you with that too." |
| "Competitor X has this." | "Thanks, that's useful context, and I've included it with your request." (Never criticize competitors.) |

## After you send

**Feedback log entry:**

> (DATE) · (CUSTOMER NAME OR ID) · Request: (EXACT WORDS) · Use case: (WHY) · Plan: (PLAN) · Ticket: (TICKET REFERENCE)
> 

**Tags:** feature request or feedback, product area. **Status:** Solved.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: count requests, not just log them. "23 customers asked for a dark mode this month" is a number the client can act on. One line each in the log makes that count possible.
