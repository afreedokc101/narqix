<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** collect missing receipts, forms or documents from the client, their staff or outside parties in as few messages as possible.

**Use it when:** your document tracker or Missing list shows open items (receipts for card transactions, W-9 forms, insurance certificates, signed forms).

**Goal:** every missing document received, or a clear reason why not.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Document tracker updated: what is missing, since when, from whom
- [ ]  Items grouped by person (one message per person, not per item)
- [ ]  Exact details for each item (date, amount, merchant, document name)
- [ ]  A safe way to send it back (reply, upload folder link shared with named people only)
- [ ]  Batch timing: twice a week at most for the same person

## The flow

### 1. Subject line with the count

> **You:** 4 receipts needed for October, by Friday
> 

*Why it works: the person knows the size of the ask before they open it.*

### 2. Short, specific list

> **You:** Hi (STAFF FIRST NAME),
To close October's books, I need receipts for these 4 card transactions:
1. 3 Oct, (MERCHANT NAME), 86.40 USD
2. 9 Oct, (MERCHANT NAME), 212.00 USD
3. 15 Oct, (MERCHANT NAME), 39.99 USD
4. 22 Oct, (MERCHANT NAME), 1,150.00 USD
> 

*Why it works: date, merchant and amount let the person find each receipt in seconds.*

### 3. How to send, and the deadline

> **You:** A photo is fine. Reply to this email, or drop them into the (FOLDER NAME) folder. Could you send them by Friday, 5:00 PM ET?
If you cannot find one, just tell me which, and I will note it for (BOOKKEEPER OR CLIENT NAME).
Thank you,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

*Why it works: an easy way out ("tell me which") gets answers instead of silence.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the person replies &quot;I lost it&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Thanks for letting me know. I have marked it as "Receipt missing" for (BOOKKEEPER NAME) to decide how to handle it.
> 

Never create, edit or "recreate" a receipt. Note it as missing.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the request is to an outside party (vendor forms, certificates)</summary>

> **You:** Hi (VENDOR CONTACT NAME), to complete your setup with (COMPANY NAME) we still need: 1) a completed W-9 form, 2) a current insurance certificate. Could you send both by (DATE)? You can reply to this email with the files.
> 

Follow the Follow Up Nudges cadence if there is no reply.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If someone sends sensitive data in the body of an email (full card number, bank login, ID number)</summary>

Do not copy it anywhere. Follow the client's process to delete or redact it, tell your Narqix lead, and reply:

> **You:** Thank you. For your security, please do not send card numbers or passwords by email. I only need the receipt itself.
>

</details>

## After you send

- Tracker updated: "Requested (DATE), batch 1".
- Received items saved with the Narqix file name and marked received.
- Items still missing at month-end listed in the month-end pack for the bookkeeper.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: one message with four items gets answered. Four messages with one item each get ignored. Batch by person, list the details, and make it easy.

</div>
