<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** confirm a refund clearly, with honest timing.

**Use it when:** the refund meets policy, passes all checks, and is within your authority (or has been approved).

**Goal:** the customer knows the amount, where it is going, and when to expect it.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Decision path complete (see Refunds and Exceptions): no chargeback, order paid, not already refunded, policy met, no abuse flags, within authority
- [ ]  Refund **actually issued** in the system before you say it is done
- [ ]  Amount and last four digits of the original payment method noted
- [ ]  Return needed or not, per policy
- [ ]  Every other question in the message noted

## The flow

### 1. Personal line and the answer

> **You:** Hi (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME),
Thanks for letting me know the (PRODUCT) wasn't right. I've refunded (AMOUNT) to your card ending in (LAST FOUR DIGITS).
> 

*Why it works: the outcome comes first, with exact amount and destination.*

### 2. Honest timing

> **You:** Refunds usually show in your account within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Your confirmation number is (REFUND REFERENCE).
> 

*Why it works: honest timing prevents "where is my refund?" tickets.*

### 3. Any next step for them

> **You:** You don't need to send the item back. (Or: Please use the return label attached within 14 days. The refund is already done, so there's nothing else to wait for.)
> 

### 4. Close

> **You:** If you don't see it by (DATE PLUS 10 BUSINESS DAYS), reply here and I'll trace it with our payments team.
Best,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the refund will be issued after the return arrives</summary>

> **You:** Here's your prepaid return label: (LABEL LINK). Once the item reaches us (usually (NUMBER) days after drop-off), I'll refund (AMOUNT) to your card ending in (LAST FOUR DIGITS), and you'll get an email confirming it.
> 

Status: Pending until the customer ships, then On-hold until received. Set a reminder.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If it is a partial refund</summary>

> **You:** I've refunded (PARTIAL AMOUNT) for the (ITEM), which is the part of your order that was affected. The rest of your order stays as it is.
> 

Explain clearly what the amount covers. Never leave the customer guessing why it is not the full order total.

</details>

## Objections and tricky replies

| Customer says | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Why does it take so long?" | "The refund left us today. After that, it's your bank's processing time, which is usually 5 to 10 business days. If it's not there by (DATE), I'll trace it for you." |
| "My card is cancelled." | "Card issuers usually still pass refunds to your account. If it doesn't arrive, contact your bank with the reference (REFUND REFERENCE), and reply here if you need help." |
| "Refund it to a different card." | "For your security, refunds can only go back to the original payment method." |

## After you send

**Internal note:**

> Issue: refund request, (REASON). Checked: decision path complete, within Tier 1 limit. Done: refunded (AMOUNT) to card ending (LAST FOUR), ref (REFUND REFERENCE). Promised: arrives in 5 to 10 business days. Next: none.
> 

**Tags:** refund, reason tag. **Status:** Solved. Log in the exception log if outside standard policy.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never write "I've refunded" before the refund is issued in the system, and never say "instantly" or "within 24 hours" unless the client's payment process guarantees it.

</div>
