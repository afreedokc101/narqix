<div class="callout callout-intro">

Purpose: ready-to-send emails, messages, prerecorded voicemail drops and cold texts for every common Work Order Desk situation, on both sides of the industry.

When to use: after a call (to confirm in writing), when the client prefers written updates, or when a phone call is not needed.

Goal of each email: one clear message, one clear ask, and a written record.

Version: v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Right recipient, right client, right campaign mailbox (never mix clients in one email)
- [ ]  Order number and address in the subject line wherever an order is involved
- [ ]  One topic per email. Several orders on one topic is fine; several topics is not.
- [ ]  Every placeholder replaced. Search the draft for "(" before sending.
- [ ]  Attachments checked: right photos, right order, readable
- [ ]  No codes, tax IDs or bank details in the email body
- [ ]  Tone: polite, factual, short

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never send lockbox codes, key codes, tax ID numbers or bank details by plain email. Never copy one client's data into another client's email. Never send an email with a placeholder still in it.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: write the subject line so the reader can act without opening the email. "Extension request: Order 4471820, 12 Oak St, new date 10/22" gets a faster answer than "Question about an order".

</div>

## Side B: vendor recruiting voicemail drops and cold texts

Use these when you recruit contractors from a cold list for a county that needs coverage. A prerecorded drop goes out when a recruiting call reaches voicemail, and a short cold text follows a few minutes later.

- [ ]  The contractor list is the one your campaign approved, with do-not-contact numbers removed
- [ ]  It is the business's normal working hours in their time zone
- [ ]  Drops are recorded once from the client's approved wording, under 25 seconds, with the callback number said twice
- [ ]  The first text to a number ends with "Reply STOP to opt out"
- [ ]  One drop and one cold text per contractor per day at most. Log both in the tracker

<details class="toggle"><summary>D1: Prerecorded drop, new county</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We're a property preservation company, and we're looking for reliable contractors in (COUNTY NAME) for grass cuts, cleanouts, securing and repairs. If you'd like steady work orders in your area, call us at (PHONE). Again, that's (PHONE). If you'd rather not hear from us, call that number and we'll take you off our list. Thank you."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>D2: Prerecorded drop, urgent coverage</summary>

> "Hi, this is (CALLER NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We have work orders ready now in (COUNTY NAME) and we're adding contractors this week. If you do (SERVICE TYPE), call us at (PHONE). That's (PHONE). To stop hearing from us, just let us know at that number. Thanks."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C1: Cold text, first touch</summary>

> "Hi, this is (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We're looking for reliable contractors in (COUNTY NAME) for preservation work orders. Are you taking on new work? Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C2: Cold text, owner name on file</summary>

> "Hi (OWNER NAME), (YOUR NAME) from (COMPANY NAME). We have (SERVICE TYPE) work orders in (COUNTY NAME). Would you like details on joining our vendor network? Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>C3: Cold text, after a voicemail drop</summary>

> "Hi, (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We just left you a short voicemail about preservation work in (COUNTY NAME). Is now a good time for a quick call? Reply STOP to opt out."
>

</details>

If a contractor replies STOP or asks not to be contacted, mark them do not contact in the tracker the same day and send nothing more.

## Common reply situations

| They reply | Your next step |
| --- | --- |
| No reply in 1 business day (vendor) or the client's normal time (client) | Send one short follow-up on the same thread, then call |
| Reply asks a question you cannot answer | "Thank you, I'll check with my team and reply by (TIME)." Then do it. |
| Reply is angry | Do not reply in the same tone. Acknowledge, state facts, offer the next step. Copy your Team Lead if it continues. |
| Reply asks for codes or sensitive data | Use only the client's secure method. Never reply with the data. |
| Reply from the wrong person | Thank them and ask who handles it; update the contact in the tracker. |

## Check yourself

<details class="toggle"><summary>1. What must be in the subject line of any email about an order?</summary>

The order number and the property address (and, where useful, the ask, like "extension request" and the new date).

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>2. A crew packet needs the lockbox code. Do you put it in the text?</summary>

No. Codes go only in the secure order notes in the app or portal, never in a text or plain email.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>3. What do you search for before sending any template?</summary>

An opening parenthesis "(" to make sure every placeholder has been replaced.

</details>
