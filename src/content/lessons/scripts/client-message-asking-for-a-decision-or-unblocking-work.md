<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** get a fast decision from the client when you are blocked or need approval.

**Use it when:** you have been blocked for 30 minutes, need approval for something risky, or have questions on a task.

**Goal:** a one-word reply ("yes", "1", "go") that lets you continue.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  You tried to solve it yourself (Client Workflow Record, past emails, task card)
- [ ]  You can state the problem in one sentence
- [ ]  You have two or three options and a recommendation
- [ ]  You know the deadline that depends on the answer
- [ ]  All questions batched into one message

## The flow

### 1. Context in one line

> **You:** Hi (CLIENT FIRST NAME), two quick decisions on the invoice batch, so I can finish by 3:00 PM ET.
> 

*Why it works: the client knows how many questions and why they matter before reading further.*

### 2. Numbered questions, each with a recommendation

> **You:**
1. Invoice from (SUPPLIER NAME), 1,240 USD, has no purchase order. My suggestion: hold it and ask (MANAGER NAME) to confirm. OK?
2. (SUPPLIER NAME) billed 18 units, but the delivery note shows 16. My suggestion: enter as billed and flag for your review, so the payment is not delayed. Or should I hold it?
> 

*Why it works: numbered items let the client answer "1 yes, 2 hold" in seconds.*

### 3. What happens if there is no answer

> **You:** If I do not hear back by 2:00 PM ET, I will hold both and finish the rest of the batch.
> 

*Why it works: a safe default keeps work moving without taking risk.*

## The read back (for risky actions)

Anything involving money, deletion, mass sending or legal documents needs a read back, even when the instruction seems clear.

> **You:** Confirming before I start: send the price update email to all 340 active customers tomorrow, Thursday, at 9:00 AM ET, using the draft you approved today. Correct?
> 

Wait for a written "yes". A call or voice note must be followed by a written confirmation.

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the client replies &quot;do whatever you think&quot;</summary>

> **You:** Will do. I will go with my suggestions. For next time, would you like me to always hold invoices without a PO? I can add it to your rules.
> 

Update the Client Workflow Record if they agree.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the client does not reply and the deadline is close</summary>

Follow the urgent path in the Client Workflow Record (for example a team channel mention or a phone call). If it is P2 or above, follow the Escalation Ladder in Narqix Standards and tell your Narqix lead.

</details>

## Bad versus good

| Bad | Good |
| --- | --- |
| "Hi" (then waiting) | One complete message with context and questions |
| "What should I do with the invoices?" | "Hold the one without a PO? My suggestion: yes." |
| Five messages over an hour | One numbered message |
| "Please advise" | "Reply 1 or 2" |

## After you send

- Note the open questions in your tracker with the time asked.
- When answered, act the same shift and confirm: "Done: held 1, entered 2 with flag."
- If the answer creates a new rule, update the Client Workflow Record and its change log.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never treat silence as approval for anything risky. No reply means you take the safe option (hold, wait, do not send), never the risky one.

</div>
