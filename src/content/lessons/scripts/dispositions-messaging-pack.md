<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** Ready-to-use emails, texts, live voicemails and prerecorded voicemail drops for every step of marketing a deal to investor buyers, from cold lists to VIP buyers.

**Use it when:** You build the buyer list, announce a deal, follow up, update buyers on status, or ask for feedback. Covers Email and Voicemail and Text.

**Goal of every message:** One clear action from the right buyer (call back, join the VIP list, book a walkthrough, send an offer, reply with feedback), sent fast.

**Track:** Real Estate  ·  **Role:** Dispositions  ·  **Time to complete:** 40 min  ·  **Version:** v1.0

</div>

## How to use this pack

Every template has purple placeholders. Replace every one before you send. A message with a placeholder still in it tells the buyer you are careless with details, and buyers judge your deals by your details.

The pack follows the life of a deal:

1. **Welcome** (new buyers and VIP sign-ups)
2. **Deal announcement** (email blast, text alert, VIP first look, live voicemail, voicemail drop)
3. **Cold buyer lists** (first-touch texts and voicemail drops to buyers you have not spoken to yet)
4. **Follow-up** (reminders, price updates, deadlines)
5. **Status updates** (under contract, back on market, closed)
6. **Feedback** (why buyers passed, buy box refresh)

## Rules before you send anything

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Send only through your client's texting platform and voicemail drop system, under your client's business name, never from a personal phone.** Check the internal do-not-contact list before every send.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Check the state before you market a deal.** Some states restrict marketing a property you only have under contract. South Carolina does not allow unlicensed people to use property details (photos, address, square footage, condition) in marketing, and a disclaimer does not fix it. Other states require licensing (for example Pennsylvania, and Illinois after one deal in 12 months) or disclosure of the equitable interest (for example Texas). Check the state's Market Compliance Card for every deal. Follow your client's instructions and title company guidance.

</div>

**Every text must:**

- Name the business in the message: "(COMPANY NAME): ..."
- Include a way to stop: "Reply STOP to opt out."
- Be sent inside the buyer's local permitted hours. A safe default is 9 AM to 8 PM their time, Monday to Saturday, with stricter state rules applied where needed.
- Stay short: one deal, one action.

**Every email must:**

- Have a subject line that sells the deal in one glance: area, numbers, and the reason to act now.
- Include your client's business name and physical mailing address.
- Include a working unsubscribe link, and unsubscribe requests must be honored promptly.
- Include the assignment disclosure line (below) on every deal email.
- Use "estimated" for ARV, repairs and profit.

**Every voicemail drop must:**

- Name the business and your name in the first sentence.
- Give one reason to call back, and the number twice.
- Stay under 30 seconds.
- Go out inside the buyer's local permitted hours, the same as texts.

**The assignment disclosure line** (adapt only with your client's approval):

> This is an assignment of contract. (COMPANY NAME) holds an equitable interest in this property under a purchase agreement and is not the owner of record. All figures are estimates. Buyers should verify all information independently.
> 

**Opt-outs:** When a buyer replies STOP (or any other opt-out word such as QUIT, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, OPT OUT), or writes anything like "remove me" or "don't text me," add them to the internal do-not-contact list immediately for that channel. Send at most one confirmation message. Never text them again.

## 3. Cold buyer lists

Use these for buyers you have pulled from property records and have not spoken to yet (recent cash buyers, landlords with several properties). The goal is one callback or reply, then the Buyer Qualification Call.

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>3A. Text: first touch to a cold buyer</strong></summary>

> (COMPANY NAME): Hi (BUYER FIRST NAME), it's (YOUR NAME). Are you still buying investment properties in (MARKET)? We lock up 30 to 40 off-market deals a month and have one in (AREA OR ZIP CODE) now. Reply YES for details. Reply STOP to opt out.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>3B. Text: cold buyer, deal-first version</strong></summary>

> (COMPANY NAME): Off-market (BEDS)/(BATHS) in (AREA OR ZIP CODE), (ASSIGNMENT PRICE), ARV est. (ARV). Want the photos and numbers? Reply YES. Reply STOP to opt out.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>3C. Text: cold buyer, VIP list invite</strong></summary>

> (COMPANY NAME): Hi (BUYER FIRST NAME), we send our VIP buyers off-market deals in (MARKET) 48 hours before our full list. Want to see if you qualify? Reply YES and I'll call you. Reply STOP to opt out.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>3D. Prerecorded voicemail drop: cold buyer list</strong></summary>

> Hi, this is (YOUR NAME) calling from (COMPANY NAME).
I see you've bought investment property in (MARKET), and I'm calling to see if you're still buying. We acquire deeply discounted, off-market deals for flippers and buy-and-hold investors, about 30 to 40 a month, and I have one right now I think you'd like.
Our VIP buyers see every deal 48 hours before our full list. Call me back at (YOUR PHONE NUMBER) to see if you qualify. Again, (YOUR PHONE NUMBER). Thanks.
>

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>3E. Text: follow-up to a cold buyer who did not reply</strong></summary>

> (COMPANY NAME): Hi (BUYER FIRST NAME), following up. Still buying in (MARKET)? New deals this week. Reply YES and I'll send them. Reply STOP to opt out.
> 

Send two to three days after 3A or 3D. When a cold buyer replies YES or calls back, run the Buyer Qualification Call the same day.

</details>

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Pair every cold voicemail drop with a text the same day. Buyers who ignore one channel often answer the other. Every reply goes to the CRM, and every YES gets a call within the hour.

</div>

## 6. Feedback

<details class="toggle"><summary><strong>6A. Email: buyer feedback request (after they passed or did not offer)</strong></summary>

**Subject:** Quick question about (PROPERTY ADDRESS)

Hi (BUYER FIRST NAME),

I noticed (PROPERTY ADDRESS) wasn't the right fit for you. Could you tell me why, in one line? It helps me send you better deals.

Just reply with a letter:

- **A.** Price too high
- **B.** ARV too high
- **C.** Repairs higher than estimated
- **D.** Location
- **E.** Property type or size
- **F.** Timing, I'm not buying right now
- **G.** Something else: (BLANK FOR BUYER)

Thank you,

(YOUR NAME) · (YOUR PHONE NUMBER)

(COMPANY NAME) · (COMPANY MAILING ADDRESS)

Unsubscribe: (UNSUBSCRIBE LINK)

*Why it works: a one-letter reply takes five seconds. Long surveys get ignored.*

</details>

## Sending schedule for one deal

| When | What | To whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every week, ongoing | 3A to 3E cold texts and 3D cold voicemail drops | Cold buyers pulled from property records |
| Day 0, morning | 2C VIP first look text, 2F VIP voicemail drop, and calls to A buyers | VIP buyers matching the buy box |
| Day 2 (48 hours later) | 2A email blast, 2B text alert, 2E voicemail drop | Full buyer list |
| Day 2 to 3 | 2D live voicemails while calling | Matching A and B buyers who did not answer |
| Day 3 | 4A follow-up email, 4B follow-up text | Buyers who opened, clicked or replied |
| Day before walkthrough | 4C reminder text | Booked buyers |
| Walkthrough day | 6B feedback text, 6C voicemail | Buyers who attended |
| Before best and final | 4E best and final reminder | Buyers who walked or asked |
| After acceptance | 5A and 5B under contract updates | Everyone who showed interest |
| If buyer fails | 5C back on the market | Interested buyers, backups first |
| After closing | 5D closed update and 6A feedback | Interested buyers |

Also post each deal to your client's investor groups and deal-listing hubs on the same day as the full list blast, if your client uses them.

## Common mistakes

1. Sending the full list blast before the VIP 48 hours are up. VIP buyers stop trusting the head start.
2. Leaving a placeholder in the message.
3. Writing subject lines like "HOT DEAL!!! WON'T LAST" (spam-flagged, and it reads like an amateur).
4. Sending property details into a state where marketing them is restricted.
5. A voicemail drop without the call-back number said twice.
6. Never telling interested buyers when a deal goes under contract.
7. Sending a follow-up text to someone who already replied STOP on another deal.
8. Letting a cold buyer's YES sit for hours. Call within the hour.

## After sending

Log in the CRM the same day:

- **Deal ID and template used** (for example 2A, 2B)
- **Audience:** buyer segment and count
- **Sent at:** date and time, with the recipients' time zones checked
- **Replies:** walkthroughs booked, offers, questions, opt-outs
- **Voicemail drops:** list, count, call-backs received
- **Opt-outs processed:** number and confirmation that each was added to the do-not-contact list
