<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** reconnect with a homeowner who asked about solar weeks or months ago and never booked.

**When to use:** leads about 30 days old or more, only when consent is on file and the DNC check is clean.

**Goal of the call:** find out what changed, re-qualify, and book one consultation with every decision maker.

**Version:** v1.0

Track: Solar  ·  Role: Appointment Setter, Lead Manager  ·  Time to complete: 20 min to learn

</div>

## Before you dial

- [ ]  Consent source and date checked. Aged leads carry more consent risk. If the record is unclear, do not dial
- [ ]  DNC scrub date checked, no flag on the national or internal list
- [ ]  Homeowner's local time inside 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday (stricter state rules apply)
- [ ]  Attempt count under 3 in the last 24 hours
- [ ]  Previous notes read: what was said before, past objections, past appointments
- [ ]  Calendar open with two real slots in the next 48 to 72 hours

<div class="callout callout-tip">

**What is different about aged leads:** contact rates are much lower, many homeowners have forgotten the inquiry, some have already gone solar or moved, and some have a new reason to look again (a higher bill, a new car, a roof replaced). Your opening must be honest about the time gap and curious about what changed.

</div>

## The flow

### 1. Open honestly

> **You:** "Hi, is this (HOMEOWNER NAME)?"
**Them:** "Yes."
**You:** "Hi (HOMEOWNER NAME), this is (YOUR NAME) with (COMPANY NAME). We install solar in (STATE). A while back, in (LEAD MONTH), you asked about solar for your home, and I am checking in to see where things stand. Just so you know, this call may be recorded for quality. Is now an okay time for a couple of minutes?"
> 

*Why it works: it names the real time gap. Pretending the inquiry was recent damages trust immediately.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;I do not remember that.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "That is completely fair, it was a few months ago. Our record shows a request from this number through (LEAD SOURCE) in (LEAD MONTH). Are you still curious about solar for your home, or should I close your file?"
> 

If they want the file closed or seem annoyed: "Done. Sorry for the bother." Code DNC if they ask not to be contacted, otherwise NI.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;I never signed up for anything.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "I am sorry for the confusion. I will remove your number now so you are not contacted again."
> 

Code DNC, note the details, flag the lead source to your team lead. Never argue.

</details>

### 2. Find out what changed

> **You:** "When you first looked into it, what got you interested?"
> 

> **You:** "And what happened since then? Did you get busy, or did something make you hold off?"
> 

*Why it works: you learn the real reason they paused. Most aged leads did not say no, they just got busy.*

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;We already went solar with another company.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "Congratulations, I hope it is working well for you. I will update our records so we do not bother you again."
> 

If your client sells batteries or expansion and the brief allows it: "Out of interest, did you add a battery with it?" One question only. Code DQ-SOLAR or note interest in batteries.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;We moved&quot; or &quot;We sold the house.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "Thanks for letting me know. Do you own your new home?"
> 

If yes, and the new address is inside the service area, re-qualify from stage 3 with the new address. If not, code DQ-MOVING or DQ-AREA and thank them.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;We got a quote and it was too expensive.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "That happens a lot. Every home is different, and options and equipment change. Would a second look with fresh numbers be useful, just so you can compare? There is no obligation."
> 

See Objections 7 and 22 in the handbook.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If they say: &quot;We were waiting to see what happened with prices.&quot;</summary>

> **You:** "That makes sense. A consultation just shows what things look like for your home today. Then you can decide with real numbers instead of guesses."
> 

No urgency claims. No mention of deadlines.

</details>

### 3. Re-qualify (things change)

Even if old notes show the answers, confirm them. Bills, roofs and households change.

> **You:** "Just to make sure my notes are current, is the home still in your name?"
> 

> **You:** "About what does the electric bill run now, on average across summer and winter?"
> 

> **You:** "Has anything changed with the roof since then? Any repairs or a new roof?"
> 

> **You:** "Still the same power company?"
> 

> **You:** "Anything new at home that uses more power, like an electric car, a pool or more people working from home?"
> 

> **You:** "And who else would want to be part of a decision like this?"
> 

Ask the shade, HOA, property type and credit questions only if they are missing from the record or may have changed (credit only if the client allows it).

### 4. Bridge

> **You:** "Thanks. It sounds like a fresh look could be worth it, especially with (THEIR REASON IN THEIR WORDS). The consultant will go through your current usage, your roof and the options available today. If it does not make sense, they will say so."
> 

### 5. Book with two choices

> **You:** "(CONSULTANT NAME) has (DAY 1) at (TIME 1) or (DAY 2) at (TIME 2). Which is better for you and (SPOUSE NAME)?"
> 

### 6. Lock it and confirm

> **You:** "Great. So that is (APPOINTMENT DAY) at (APPOINTMENT TIME), your time, at (PROPERTY ADDRESS), with you and (SPOUSE NAME). Please have a recent electric bill ready. Can I text a confirmation to this number? I will also call the day before to check the time still works."
> 

### 7. Close or move to nurture

If they are not ready but open:

> **You:** "No problem at all. Would it be okay if I checked in again around (NURTURE DATE)?"
> 

Code NUR-(REASON) with the date.

If they decline:

> **You:** "I understand. Thank you for your time, and take care."
>

## After the call

> **(DISPOSITION) / Aged lead from (LEAD SOURCE) (LEAD DATE)**
What changed since inquiry: (THEIR WORDS).
Re-qualified: owner (YES OR NO), avg bill about (AMOUNT), roof (DETAIL), utility (NAME), DMs (NAMES).
Next: (NEXT STEP AND DATE). (YOUR NAME)
> 

If SET, use the full standard set note and send the written confirmation. Update any field that changed since the original inquiry.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** On aged lists, a "no" is often just "not then". Your curiosity question ("what happened since then?") is the most valuable line in this script. Listen for a new reason to act, like a new car or a higher summer bill, and connect the consultation to that reason.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:**

Never pretend you spoke with the homeowner before if you did not.

Never dial an aged lead with unclear consent or an old DNC scrub.

Never use "prices are going up" or "incentives are ending" to create urgency.

</div>
