<div class="callout callout-intro">

**Purpose:** keep a promise to update the customer, even when there is nothing new to report.

**Use it when:** you (or a teammate) promised an update by a time, and the answer from the carrier, warehouse, product team or client has not arrived.

**Goal:** the customer feels looked after, and knows the next update time.

**Version:** v1.0

</div>

## Before you send

- [ ]  Checked one more time for news (carrier, internal team, client)
- [ ]  Chased the internal team or vendor before writing to the customer
- [ ]  A new, realistic update time set
- [ ]  Sending **before** the promised time passes

## The flow

### 1. Personal line and honest status

> **You:** Hi (CUSTOMER FIRST NAME),
As promised, here's an update on (ISSUE). I don't have a final answer yet from (TEAM OR CARRIER), and I didn't want you to wonder whether we'd forgotten.
> 

*Why it works: honesty plus reliability. Customers value being kept informed more than they mind waiting.*

### 2. What you did today

> **You:** I followed up with them again this morning, and they've told me (ANY DETAIL, for example "the claim is being reviewed by their investigations team").
> 

### 3. Next update time

> **You:** I'll update you again by (TIME AND ZONE), sooner if I hear anything.
Best,
(YOUR NAME)
> 

<details class="toggle"><summary>If this is the second or third &quot;no news&quot; update</summary>

Add a concrete step forward, not just another wait:

> **You:** This has taken longer than it should. I've escalated it to (SENIOR TEAM OR CLIENT), and if (CARRIER) hasn't replied by (DATE), we'll (RESEND / REFUND) under our policy so you're not left waiting any longer.
> 

Check the matrix before committing to the step.

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>If the news arrives while you are writing</summary>

Throw away the draft. Send the real answer using the right reply for the outcome.

</details>

## Objections and tricky replies

| Customer says | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "So nothing's happening?" | "It's moving, just slower than I'd like. Here's what's happened so far: (STEPS). Next update by (TIME AND ZONE)." |
| "Stop updating me and just fix it." | "Understood. I'll only contact you when I have the answer, and no later than (DATE)." Note their preference. |
| "I want it resolved today." | If the matrix allows an immediate resolution (resend or refund), offer it. If not, escalate with the customer's request noted. |

## After you send

**Internal note:**

> Update sent (TIME AND ZONE), no news from (TEAM). Chased (TEAM) (TIME). Next update promised by (TIME AND ZONE).
> 

**Status:** On-hold. Reminder set for the new time.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never let a promised update time pass in silence. "I'll update you Thursday" means Thursday, with or without news. A missed promise turns a patient customer into an angry one.

</div>
