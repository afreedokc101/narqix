## What the Narqix Standards are

These are the rules that make Narqix different from a typical outsourcing provider. They apply to every person, on every campaign, in every country. A client in Texas and a client in London get the same standard of conduct, security, communication and quality.

The standards are written to be testable. Each rule says what to do, by when, and how it is checked. If a rule is unclear, tell your Campaign Lead so we can make it clearer.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Clients rarely judge us on our best day. They judge us on our most ordinary Tuesday: was the report there, was the reply fast, were the notes clean? The standards exist to make every ordinary day excellent.

</div>

## How the standards fit with your campaign

- **Narqix Standards** set the minimum for everyone.
- **Your Client Playbook** adds client-specific detail: tools, authority limits, tone, approved scripts.
- When the Playbook is stricter, follow the Playbook.
- When the Playbook seems to allow something a Narqix standard forbids, stop and ask your Campaign Lead. Red rules are never relaxed by a client request.

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never treat a standard as optional because "nobody checks". QA samples work every week, clients can see tracker data, and compliance mistakes can surface months later in a complaint or lawsuit.

</div>
