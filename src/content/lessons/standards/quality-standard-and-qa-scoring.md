## What quality means at Narqix

Quality is work that is **correct, complete, on time, done the right way, and safe for the client's customer to see**. Speed and volume matter, but never at the cost of these five.

QA (quality assurance) is not a punishment. It is how Narqix proves to clients that our work is reliable, and how you get specific, useful coaching every week.

## How work is sampled

| Who | What is reviewed |
| --- | --- |
| Every team member | At least **5 items per week**, chosen at random (calls, chats, emails, tickets, tasks or CRM records) |
| First 30 days on a campaign | At least **10 items per week** |
| New team member, customer-facing writing | **100% of your first 20** customer-facing messages are reviewed, often before sending |
| Always included | Every escalation, every customer complaint, every negative customer rating |

QA Specialists score the same sample items together once a month and compare results. This keeps scoring fair and consistent between reviewers.

## The scorecards

Use the scorecard for your work type. Each item is scored, weighted, and added up to a percentage.

### Calls (outbound and inbound)

| Criterion | Weight | What a full score looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening and disclosures | 10% | Name, company, purpose and recording disclosure, as scripted |
| Listening and discovery | 20% | Asked the right questions, let the person finish, followed up on what they said |
| Accuracy | 15% | Correct information about the client's offer, process and next steps |
| Tone and clarity | 15% | Calm, warm, clear pace, easy to understand |
| Call control and next step | 15% | Kept the call on track, agreed a clear next step with a time |
| CRM notes and disposition | 15% | Correct disposition code, notes in the Narqix format, entered right after the call |
| Script and process | 10% | Followed the approved flow, used approved answers to objections |

**Pass mark: 85%.** Below 70% means coaching within 3 working days.

### Written customer replies (email, tickets, texts)

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Every question answered; issue resolved or next step owned | 25% |
| Accuracy (policy, prices, order or file details checked) | 20% |
| Tone and personal touch | 15% |
| Structure and clarity | 10% |
| Grammar, spelling, correct names, no unfilled placeholders | 10% |
| Next step: who does what, by when | 10% |
| Process: tags, status, notes, verification | 10% |

**Pass mark: 88%.** Complaints and sensitive replies: **90%**.

### Chat

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting within 30 seconds, using their name and issue | 10% |
| Pace: no silent gaps, check-in times stated and kept | 15% |
| Resolution | 25% |
| Accuracy | 15% |
| Short-form tone | 15% |
| Saved replies personalised | 10% |
| Closing with outcome and next step | 10% |

**Pass mark: 85%.**

### Back office tasks (data, inbox, calendar, research, documents)

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy against the source | 35% |
| Completeness | 20% |
| Timeliness | 15% |
| Process (SOP followed, naming, logging) | 15% |
| Communication (update sent, questions batched) | 15% |

**Pass mark: 90%.** Below 80% means coaching.

## Auto-fails

These score **zero** for the item, no matter how good the rest was:

- Customer or client data shared with the wrong person, or put into an unapproved tool
- A required verification step skipped (for example, identity check before changing account details)
- False or misleading information given to a customer or client
- Rude, dismissive or discriminatory language
- A missed compliance step: recording disclosure not given, a stop or do-not-call request not honoured, a call outside permitted hours
- A refund, credit or promise above your authority without approval
- A bulk change (many records, mass message) made without approval
- A promise you knew would not be kept
- Invented facts in research or reports

<div class="callout callout-never">

**Never do this:** Never hide, delete or re-do an item to avoid a QA review. Never coach a customer to give a good rating. Gaming QA is treated as dishonesty.

</div>

## Self-QA: the five-point check before "done"

Run this check before you mark anything complete.

1. **Request:** does it match exactly what was asked?
2. **Source:** did I verify it against the original source?
3. **Place:** is it in the right place, with the right name and permissions?
4. **Record:** is the CRM, tracker or report updated?
5. **Customer test:** would I be comfortable if the client's customer saw this?

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Top performers review their own recordings. Once a week, listen to two of your calls or reread five of your messages, and score them yourself on the scorecard before QA does. You will find most of your coaching points before anyone else does, and your scores will rise fast.

</div>

## After your score

- **Pass:** your QA Specialist or lead shares what you did well. Keep doing it.
- **Below pass:** a 15-minute coaching session within 3 working days. You will see the exact moment or line, hear or see the correct version, and practise it.
- **If the SOP or script caused the miss:** the SOP is fixed, and your score is reviewed.
- **Two weeks below target:** a written improvement plan with clear goals (see Performance Reviews and Coaching).

## Disputing a score

You may dispute a score within **3 working days**. Send your Campaign Lead: the item, the criterion, and your reason in two or three sentences. A different QA reviewer will check it. Disputes that are specific and respectful are welcome. They make our scoring better.

## Check yourself

2. What is the pass mark for written customer replies, and for complaint replies?
3. Name three auto-fails.
4. List the five-point self-check.
5. You think a score is wrong. What do you send, to whom, and within how long?
