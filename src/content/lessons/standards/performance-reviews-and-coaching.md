## Why Narqix coaches every week

Most companies review people once a year. By then, small habits have become big problems. At Narqix, coaching is weekly, short and specific. You always know where you stand, and nothing in a formal review should ever surprise you.

## The review rhythm

| When | What | With whom | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Every shift | Start and end-of-day reports; quick feedback in the team channel | Campaign Lead | A few minutes |
| Every week | Scorecard review: KPIs, QA scores, one strength, one focus area | Campaign Lead | 15 minutes |
| After every QA score below pass | Coaching session on that exact item | Campaign Lead or QA Specialist | 15 minutes, within 3 working days |
| Every month | One-to-one: results, quality, reliability, wellbeing, growth | Campaign Lead | 30 minutes |
| Day 30, 60 and 90 on a new campaign | Ramp-up review: are you on track to full performance? | Campaign Lead, sometimes the Operations Manager | 30 minutes |
| Every quarter | Formal review: level progress, goals for the next quarter | Campaign Lead and Operations Manager | 45 minutes |

## What you are reviewed on

The same five areas from How Narqix Works:

1. **Results:** KPIs against target.
2. **Quality:** QA average and any auto-fails.
3. **Reliability:** attendance, punctuality, reports, tracker accuracy.
4. **Communication:** response times, clarity, early escalation.
5. **Conduct and compliance:** security, compliance, honesty, professionalism.

## How a coaching session works

Narqix coaching follows six steps. Leads use them; you should know them too, so you can get the most out of every session.

1. **Look at the evidence together.** The recording, message or task, not opinions.
2. **Ask first.** "How do you think this went?" Most people spot their own issue.
3. **Name one focus.** One clear behaviour to change, not ten.
4. **Show the better version.** The lead models it: the exact words, the better note, the cleaner process.
5. **Practise it.** Role-play or redo it right there in the session.
6. **Agree and follow up.** A specific commitment ("use the read-back on every address this week") and a check next week.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Come to every coaching session with one question of your own. "What would a Senior do differently on this call?" is a great one. People who ask for feedback grow twice as fast as people who wait for it.

</div>

## How to receive feedback well

- **Listen fully** before responding. Take notes.
- **Ask for the example** if feedback feels vague: "Can you show me where that happened?"
- **Separate the work from yourself.** Feedback is about a behaviour you can change, not about who you are.
- **Repeat back** the focus area in your own words to confirm you understood.
- **Act visibly.** Use the change on your next shift and mention it in your end-of-day report.
- **Disagree respectfully** if you have evidence. You can dispute a QA score within 3 working days (see Quality Standard and QA Scoring).

## Your monthly self-review

Before each monthly one-to-one, send your Campaign Lead this short self-review:

```
Monthly self-review, (MONTH)
My results vs target: (KPI: ACTUAL vs TARGET)
My QA average: (SCORE). Best item: (WHAT WENT WELL)
One thing I improved this month: (SPECIFIC)
One thing I want to improve next month: (SPECIFIC)
Something that would help me: (TRAINING, TOOL, PROCESS CHANGE)
Wellbeing (sleep, energy, workload), 1 to 5: (NUMBER), optional comment
```

## When performance is below target

Narqix is direct and fair when performance drops.

| Stage | Trigger | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focused coaching** | One week below target, or a QA score below pass | Extra coaching, practice, and a clear focus for the next week |
| **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** | Two weeks in a row below target, or a serious conduct issue | A written plan with 2 to 3 specific, measurable goals, support offered (training, shadowing, extra QA), and weekly check-ins, usually for 30 days |
| **Plan outcome** | End of the plan | Goals met: the plan closes and you continue normally. Goals not met: you may move to a different campaign or role, or your time at Narqix may end. |

A PIP is a genuine chance to succeed, not paperwork before an exit. Many strong Narqix people have completed one.

Critical breaches (dishonesty, data misuse, harassment, serious compliance violations) may skip these stages. See Code of Conduct.

**Never do this:** Never hide a problem to protect your scores. Never ask a teammate to cover a mistake. Never discuss your review or a teammate's performance with clients.

## For Campaign Leads: coaching standards

- Weekly scorecard review with every team member, without exception.
- Coaching within 3 working days of any below-pass QA score.
- Praise in public, correct in private.
- Every piece of feedback tied to evidence and one clear next behaviour.
- Notes of every coaching session and PIP check-in kept in the team member's record.
- Fatigue and wellbeing checked monthly. Look for sleep issues before assuming effort issues.
