## What Core Skills are

Core Skills are the abilities that make someone excellent in any role, for any client. Industry knowledge changes from campaign to campaign. These skills travel with you everywhere: a clear voice on the phone, a clean CRM note, a well-written email, a correct time conversion, a calm answer to an angry customer.

They are also what clients notice first. A client may forgive a gap in product knowledge in week one. They rarely forgive a confusing email or a missed meeting caused by a time zone mistake.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

**Coach's note:** Core Skills are practised, not just read. Every page has drills. Do them out loud, on paper, or in your practice CRM. Fifteen minutes of practice a day for a month will change how clients hear and read you.

</div>

## Core scripts (Script Library, Track: Core)

These scripts apply to every calling campaign, in every industry. Track scripts are built on top of them.

| Script | Stage | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal Call Opening and Closing | Opening | Starting and ending every outbound and inbound call correctly and compliantly |
| Handling Abusive or Angry Callers | Objections | Calming an upset person, setting boundaries, and knowing when to end a call |
| Voicemail Basics | Voicemail & Text | Leaving short, clear, compliant voicemails that get callbacks |
| Stop, Do-Not-Call and Wrong Number Requests | Objections | Handling opt-outs and wrong numbers instantly and correctly |
| Hold, Transfer and Message Taking | Inbound | Putting people on hold, transferring warmly and taking complete messages |
