<div class="callout callout-intro">

For Narqix designers working on brand identity, marketing design, UI and UX, and print. Use this page as your working checklist to deliver design to the Narqix standard.

Track: Specialist  ·  Role: Designer  ·  Time to complete: 10 min

</div>

## Definition of done

- [ ]  The design solves the problem written in the brief, and you can explain how in one sentence
- [ ]  It meets every acceptance criterion in the Scope Sheet
- [ ]  All agreed sizes, formats and variations are exported and named correctly
- [ ]  Editable source files are organized and saved in the client's workspace or storage
- [ ]  Every font, image, icon and illustration is licensed for the client's use, with the license recorded
- [ ]  The client approved the final in writing

## Quality checklist

<details class="toggle"><summary>Brief and concept</summary>

- [ ]  Brief confirmed in writing: goal, audience, message, formats, deadline, approver
- [ ]  References and mood direction agreed before detailed work starts
- [ ]  Two or three distinct directions at concept stage, not small variations of one idea

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Craft</summary>

- [ ]  Clear hierarchy: the eye goes to the most important thing first
- [ ]  Consistent spacing system and alignment grid
- [ ]  Typography limited to the brand fonts, with consistent sizes and weights
- [ ]  Colors from the brand palette, with text contrast that passes WCAG AA
- [ ]  Copy proofread: spelling, names, numbers, dates, phone numbers, URLs
- [ ]  Works at the smallest size it will be seen (mobile feed, favicon, business card)
- [ ]  Checked in light and dark backgrounds where relevant

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Brand identity projects</summary>

- [ ]  Logo works in full color, one color, black and white, and at very small sizes
- [ ]  Clear space and minimum size defined
- [ ]  Palette defined with values for screen and print
- [ ]  Type system, imagery style and tone of voice examples included
- [ ]  Brand guide delivered as a short, usable document, not a showpiece
- [ ]  Basic trademark search suggested to the client before final adoption (the client's legal advisor confirms)

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>UI and UX design</summary>

- [ ]  Designed mobile first, then larger screens
- [ ]  Components and styles built as a reusable system, not one-off screens
- [ ]  Every state designed: empty, loading, error, success, disabled
- [ ]  Interactive prototype for key flows reviewed with the client
- [ ]  Developer handover includes specs, spacing, assets and notes on behavior

</details>

<details class="toggle"><summary>Files</summary>

- [ ]  Layers and pages named and organized; no "Layer 47" or "Final final 2"
- [ ]  Unused elements removed from the source file
- [ ]  Export names follow YYYY-MM-DD_Client_Asset_Size_v01
- [ ]  Print files: correct size, bleed, color mode and resolution confirmed with the printer

</details>

## Pre-launch checklist (before anything goes public or to print)

- [ ]  Final proofread by someone other than the designer, or a slow read-aloud by you
- [ ]  Every phone number, email, URL and QR code tested
- [ ]  Legal lines, disclaimers and required logos present
- [ ]  Correct formats exported for each channel
- [ ]  Print proof approved by the client before the full print run
- [ ]  Source files and licenses saved to the client's storage

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: present design inside its real context. A social post shown in a phone feed mockup, a sign shown on a storefront photo, a web page shown on a phone. Clients approve faster and give better feedback when they see how it will really be seen.

</div>

## Reporting KPIs

| KPI | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Deliverables on time | 90 percent or more |
| Revision rounds per deliverable | Within the rounds agreed in the Scope Sheet |
| Errors found after approval (typos, wrong numbers) | Zero |
| Performance of designs in use | Where available: ad click-through rate, landing page conversion, email clicks, compared with the previous design |
| Brand consistency check | Every asset passes the brand guide check before delivery |

## Access and security rules

- Work inside the client's design tool workspace or shared storage, not only in your personal account.
- Fonts, stock images and templates are purchased under the client's name or license, never your personal license.
- Never use a client's unreleased designs, logos or materials in your portfolio without written permission from the client through Narqix.
- Keep client brand assets off personal devices and personal cloud storage.
- AI image tools only through approved business accounts, and only when the client agreed to AI-generated imagery.

## Common mistakes

<details class="toggle"><summary>Open the list</summary>

- Starting to design before the brief and message are clear.
- Showing ten options instead of three strong ones.
- Using images or fonts found online with no license.
- Delivering only flat exports, leaving the client unable to edit anything.
- Designing for a large desktop screen when most people will see it on a phone.
- Low contrast text that looks elegant but cannot be read.
- Copying a well-known brand's look so closely that it creates legal risk.

</details>

## Red flags to escalate

| Red flag | Level | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client asks you to copy another company's logo, design or protected material | P2 | Decline, inform Delivery Lead |
| An asset is already live with a wrong price, phone number or legal line | P1 | Alert client and Delivery Lead immediately |
| Licensing of a font or image is unclear | P3 | Replace or confirm before delivery |
| Feedback comes from several people with conflicting directions | P3 | Ask the named approver to consolidate |
| Revisions exceed the agreed rounds | P3 | Raise a change request |

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never deliver work containing an image, font or asset you cannot prove the client is licensed to use. "I found it online" is not a license.

</div>
