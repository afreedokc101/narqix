## What this track is

Back office work is invisible when it is done well. The client opens their inbox and it is calm. Their calendar has no double bookings. The CRM shows the truth. The report on Friday has real numbers. Nobody notices the person behind it, and that is the point.

It is also the work where one small slip can do real damage: a wrong amount in a ledger, a deleted contact list, a meeting booked in the wrong time zone, a contract saved where the whole world can open it. This track teaches you the craft that prevents those slips. It is the Narqix way, and it is stricter than what most offshore firms teach.

## Learning path

Work through the pages in this order. Do not skip the procedures, even if you have done admin work before. Our standards are different.

1. **Role guide** for your role (pages 01 to 06). Read the others later so you understand your teammates.

3. **Calendar Management** (08): the Five Facts invite and the evening brief.

## The five habits of a Narqix back office specialist

1. **Every item gets one clear outcome.** Nothing sits in limbo. Every email, task and record ends a session handled, assigned, waiting or parked on purpose.
2. **Checks are structured, not visual.** "I looked it over" is not a check. We compare, count and reconcile.
3. **The client owns everything.** Their accounts, their folders, their data. You are a guest with a key, never the owner.
4. **Written down or it did not happen.** Every process is recorded, every change is logged, every promise has a date.
5. **Numbers, not activity.** Reports say what changed for the client, with numbers, not how busy you were.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never delete, bulk edit, send to a large list, move money or change a system setting because it "seemed obvious". If it cannot be undone easily, you read it back to the client and get a written yes first.

</div>
