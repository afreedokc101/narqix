For every Narqix specialist and lead. After reading you will be able to build and maintain a Client Workflow Record: the living document that lets any trained Narqix person run a client's work tomorrow without asking the client to explain it again.

Track: Back Office & Admin  ·  Role: All back office roles, Team Leads  ·  Time to complete: 30 min

</div>

## Why this is the most important document we write

When a specialist is sick, on leave, promoted or replaced, the client should feel **nothing**. No repeated explanations, no dropped tasks, no "how did the last person do this?". That promise is only possible if every client's work is written down in one place, in a way anyone trained can follow.

That place is the **Client Workflow Record**. One per client. It sits in the client's playbook folder on the shared drive, owned by the client, maintained by you.

<div class="callout callout-coach">

Coach's note: use the Backup Test. Ask yourself: "If I disappeared tonight, could a trained Narqix teammate do my job tomorrow using only this record and the logins in the password manager?" If the answer is no, the record is not done.

</div>

## What the record contains

### Part 1: Client snapshot

| Item | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Business and what it does | (COMPANY NAME), residential HVAC, 14 technicians |
| Client contacts | Owner, operations manager, bookkeeper: names, roles, preferred channel |
| Time zone and hours | Central Time, Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM CT |
| Your scheduled shift and overlap | Per the shift plan |
| Tone and sign-off | Friendly and short; sign as "(YOUR NAME), Office of (CLIENT NAME)" |
| Spelling and dates | US English, dates as Oct 18, 2026 |

### Part 2: Access map

List each system you use, what level of access you have, and where the login lives (the password manager item name). **Never write passwords in the record.**

| System | Access level | Login location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email inbox | Delegated | Your own login |
| CRM | Standard user | Password manager: "(CLIENT) CRM" |
| Shared drive | Editor on Admin, viewer on Finance | Your own login |

### Part 3: Authority and limits

- What you may do without asking
- What always needs approval
- Spending limit (if any)
- **The Never list:** things the client never wants done (for example "never reply to my mother's emails", "never book Friday afternoons", "never offer discounts")

### Part 4: VIP and escalation

- VIP people and what they get (fast alerts, never auto-replies)
- Who to contact for what, and how (team channel, phone) for urgent issues
- Narqix escalation path (link to the Escalation Ladder in Narqix Standards)

### Part 5: Task cards

This is the heart of the record. **One card per recurring task.**

> **Task card: Weekly vendor invoice batch**
**Trigger:** every Monday by 12:00 PM CT, or when 10 or more invoices are waiting
**Inputs:** invoices in the inbox folder "05 Finance" and the shared drive "Invoices In"
**Steps:**
1. Collect all new invoices since last Monday.
2. Enter in the accounting system using the Double Entry Comparison method.
3. Match each to its purchase order; no PO goes to Held.
4. Control total checked against the invoice list.
5. Post the summary in the client channel.
**Done means:** all invoices entered or held, control total matches, summary posted
**Approval needed:** none for entry; client approves payment
**Exceptions:** invoices over 5,000 USD flagged separately; new vendors flagged
**Time it takes:** about 90 minutes for 30 invoices
**Recording:** link to the walkthrough video
**Owner:** (YOUR NAME)  ·  **Backup:** (BACKUP NAME)
**Last verified:** (DATE)
> 

### Part 6: Calendar, inbox and file rules

Link to or copy the client's calendar rules (see Calendar Management), inbox folders and sweep times (see Inbox Management), and folder structure (see File Naming and Document Control).

### Part 7: Change log

| Date | What changed | Why | Approved by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (DATE) | Invoices over 5,000 USD now flagged | Client request after audit | (CLIENT NAME) |

## How to build it (first 30 days)

| When | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Kickoff | Lead fills Parts 1 to 4 with the client. You listen and note the Never list word for word. |
| Week 1 | For each task, ask the client to record a short screen walkthrough while doing it for real. Write a task card from each recording the same day. |
| Week 1 | Send the client the task cards to confirm: "Did I capture this correctly?" |
| Weeks 2 to 3 | Do the tasks from the cards. Every time reality differs from the card, fix the card. |
| Week 4 | Backup Test: your backup reads the record and walks through two task cards with you. Fix every question they ask. |
| Day 30 | Lead reviews the record with the client and agrees which tasks no longer need approval. |

## How to keep it alive

1. **Update the same day** something changes. A record that is three weeks out of date is dangerous.
2. **Review every 90 days.** Read every card. Mark "Last verified" with the date.
3. **Log every change** in Part 7.
4. **Before leave**, read the record with your backup and add anything that is only in your head.

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never put passwords, codes, full account numbers or customer personal data in the Client Workflow Record. It is shared with backups and leads. Point to the password manager item instead.

</div>

<div class="callout callout-never">

Never do this: never keep "secret knowledge" that only you know. Some people do this to feel safe in their job. At Narqix it is the opposite: specialists who document well are trusted with bigger accounts.

</div>
