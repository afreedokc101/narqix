NARQIX ACADEMY

Non-Disclosure Agreement

For Narqix Academy trainees and candidates

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Narqix Solutions Ltd ("Narqix", "we" or "us") and the person who signs it below (the "Candidate" or "you"). It takes effect on the date you sign it.

In plain words: Narqix Academy gives you our training, scripts, playbooks and exams, and you may later work on real client accounts. Everything you see stays private. You use it only to train and work with Narqix, you never share or copy it, and you never contact our clients or their customers behind our back.

1.Why we share information with you

Narqix trains candidates through Narqix Academy so that they can be considered for work with Narqix and the businesses Narqix serves ("Clients"). To do this, we share confidential material with you. You may only use it to take part in Narqix Academy and, if you are engaged, to do the work Narqix assigns to you (the "Purpose").

2.What counts as Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information you receive from or through Narqix, in any form (written, spoken, on screen, in a file or in a system), including:

  1. all Narqix Academy content: lessons, standards, scripts, playbooks, checklists, templates, role-plays, exam questions, exam answers and certification materials;
  2. Narqix's business methods, processes, systems, workflows, pricing, rates, partners, suppliers and plans;
  3. the names, details, campaigns, playbooks, results and business information of Narqix's Clients;
  4. personal information about anyone, including Clients' customers, homeowners, leads, sellers, buyers, merchants and their contact details, property details and financial information ("Personal Data");
  5. logins, passwords, access codes, lockbox codes and any information that gives access to a system, account or property.

3.What is not Confidential Information

This Agreement does not cover information that you can show (a) was already public when you received it, or later became public through no fault of yours; (b) you lawfully knew before Narqix shared it, without any duty to keep it private; or (c) you lawfully received from someone else who was free to share it. Personal Data is always treated as confidential.

4.Your promises

You agree that you will:

  1. keep all Confidential Information strictly private and use it only for the Purpose;
  2. not share it with anyone, including family, friends, other trainees outside Narqix work, other companies or social media;
  3. not copy, download, screenshot, photograph, record, print, forward or post any Academy content, exam question or Client information, except where Narqix tells you to as part of your work;
  4. not paste or upload Confidential Information into any AI tool, website or app unless Narqix or the Client has approved that tool for the task;
  5. keep your devices, accounts and passwords secure, and never let anyone else use your Narqix or Client logins;
  6. tell Narqix at hiring@narqix.com straight away if you think any Confidential Information has been lost, shared or seen by someone who should not see it.

5.Exams and certification

Narqix Academy exams and certificates show what you know yourself. You will not share exam questions or answers, look for or use leaked answers, use AI tools or help from anyone during an exam, or let anyone sit an exam for you. If you break this rule, Narqix may cancel your results, revoke any certificate and remove you from the Academy.

6.Clients and their customers

While you take part in Narqix Academy or work with Narqix, and for 12 months after that ends, you will not use Confidential Information to contact, offer services to, or work directly for any Client you learned about through Narqix, or to contact any of a Client's customers or leads outside your Narqix work. If a Client offers you work directly, you will politely decline and tell Narqix.

7.Ownership

All Confidential Information, Academy content and anything you create from it for Narqix belong to Narqix or the relevant Client. This Agreement does not give you any licence or right to use them for yourself.

8.Returning and deleting information

When Narqix asks, or when you leave the Academy or stop working with Narqix, you will return or permanently delete all Confidential Information you hold, including copies, notes and files, and confirm this in writing if asked.

9.When the law requires disclosure

If a court, government authority or the law requires you to disclose Confidential Information, you may disclose only what is required. Where the law allows, you will tell Narqix first so that it can protect the information.

10.How long this Agreement lasts

This Agreement starts when you sign it. Your duties continue for 3 years after you leave the Academy or stop working with Narqix, whichever is later. Your duties for Personal Data, passwords and trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains confidential.

11.No job offer or payment

Narqix Academy is free. Signing this Agreement, completing training or earning a certificate does not create an employment relationship and does not guarantee you a job, a client placement or any payment. Any work arrangement will be agreed separately in writing.

12.If this Agreement is broken

A breach of this Agreement may cause Narqix and its Clients serious harm. If you break it, Narqix may remove you from the Academy, revoke any certificate, end any work arrangement, and take legal action, including asking a court to stop the breach and to award damages.

13.General

  1. Governing law: this Agreement is governed by the laws of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka have jurisdiction.
  2. Whole agreement: this is the complete agreement between us about confidentiality. It can only be changed in writing signed by both of us.
  3. Severability: if any part of this Agreement cannot be enforced, the rest still applies, and that part applies as far as the law allows.
  4. Signatures: a scanned, photographed or electronically signed copy of this Agreement is as valid as the original.
